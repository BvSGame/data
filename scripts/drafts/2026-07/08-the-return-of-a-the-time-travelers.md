# The Return of (a/the) Time Traveler(s)

A script draft for _Bombs vs. Sleepwalkers_. Written in **July 26, 2026** by **liledix⁴**. It continues the story of _Mad Auntie_.

----

Flash! Bang! Saylor and Charlotte reappeared in a living room in 2024 Saylor’s rental. It’s 10 PM.

SAYLOR: (instantly faceplants onto the couch) Dear universe, the show is over! Turn off the lights!
CHARLOTTE: Saylor.
SAYLOR: (muffled) Charlie, I want to sleep. Set your PTT to go home, and fire well.
CHARLOTTE: Uh-huh. Girl, you don’t do send-off so casually, okay? If you have no strength to keep it up, I’ll take defense duties for you. You do remember we have to protect your precious magic fridge from the roaming metalheads, right?
SAYLOR: (muffled) I’ve already laid out my trap at the corridor—
CHARLOTTE: And someone has to control it! (takes Saylor’s tablet) Don’t worry, I’ll handle it. Just give my user some permissions to use your defense toolkit.
SAYLOR: (muffled) Okay, okay.

Saylor sits up, takes tablet from Charlotte’s hand, operates it for a few seconds, then hands it over to Charlotte.

SAYLOR: Here.
CHARLOTTE: In case of emergencies, I’ll connect my software to your network, is it fine?
SAYLOR: No super-user rights, but do whatever you want. Now, if you’ll excuse me, I’ve got a world to tell to go to hell. Past, present, future, no matter.

And Saylor faceplants onto the couch again. Knock-knock on the front door.

SAYLOR: (muffled) I don’t care if it’s late or early, no one’s home! My eyes are closed, and so is the door!
CHARLOTTE: Come in!

Enter Ashley, African-American student of technical university, Saylor’s university classmate.

ASHLEY: Saylor! (pauses) Oh. New face.
CHARLOTTE: That’s right! (reaches out) I’m Charlotte. Best known as Charlotte. But Saylor prefers to call me Red Alert.
SAYLOR: (muffled) I never called her like that!
ASHLEY: (shakes hands) Ashley. Or just Ash. (to Saylor) Hey Sayley, you seem to zone out a little too early.
SAYLOR: (muffled) Ashley? (slowly sitting up) What are you doing here?
ASHLEY: You forgot? An evening walk! Don’t forget our tradition. Well, everyone’s busy today, unfortunately, so I can only ask you.
SAYLOR: Ash. I’m finished.
ASHLEY: That’s not you talking.
CHARLOTTE: See? I have the same opinion. Do you mind a friendly gesture?
ASHLEY: What gesture?
CHARLOTTE: (side-hugs Ashley) This gesture! Now, Saylor, don’t be a downer. Introduce us!
ASHLEY: Yeah, please do.
CHARLOTTE: Before I did it myself, and I know you don’t like when I do it. (to Ashley, lower volume) It usually involves lightning strikes and nobody is happy.
SAYLOR: Sigh… (points at Ashley) Ashley, a fellow university student. (points at Charlotte) Charlotte, an independent time traveler. With unnaturally limitless energy.
ASHLEY: Are you?
CHARLOTTE: I have curls for a reason!
ASHLEY: Okay. I dig that. Saylor, it surprises me how you manage to find unusual friends.
CHARLOTTE: Am I unusual?
ASHLEY: Normally people don’t mix curls with this casual look, girl.
CHARLOTTE: Ah. Fair observation. I’m not a normal person, though. Normal people can’t do (singing theatrically) _hi-i-i-igh-pitch vibra-a-a-ato-o-o-o!_
ASHLEY: (genuinely) Oh dear.
CHARLOTTE: Oh yes.
ASHLEY: And what’s that sticking out from under your sleeve?
CHARLOTTE: Nothing really unusual. (unrolls her sleeve to show a strapped forearm gadget) Just a portable time transporter. PTT for short.
ASHLEY: It’s not integrated into your arm, right?
CHARLOTTE: (disapproving facial expression) Please.
SAYLOR: (to Ashley) Are you still catching up?
ASHLEY: Don’t mind me, really. (looking at Saylor) I’ve seen stranger things.
CHARLOTTE: Ooh! May I join you on your walk, please? You can tell me everything!
ASHLEY: I’m fine with that. Saylor?
SAYLOR: I want this world to forget about my existence for some hours.

Ashley and Charlotte look at each other, nod, then they lift Saylor by each arm in unison.

SAYLOR: No-no-no, stop it!
ASHLEY: I said, it’s a tradition and nobody else joined!
CHARLOTTE: Well, except me, but we can’t keep you babysitting.
SAYLOR: I’m not babysitting!
CHARLOTTE: Then prove it!
ASHLEY: Wait. Babysitting?

Stelle is slowly walking down the stairs.

STELLE: Mom? Cherry?

Ashley and Charlotte pause. Charlotte points her finger at Stelle with excitement.

CHARLOTTE: Oh! She called me Cherry!
ASHLEY: Explain.

Saylor stands up herself, then watches Charlotte deliver her explanation.

CHARLOTTE: (coughs) Well. Welcome to the mad house, my girl, because this (points at Stelle) is Saylor’s daughter from the far future.
ASHLEY: (slowly nods) Okay, keep going.
CHARLOTTE: Saylor went to the 14th millennium, ran a bioscan on the terminal unknowingly, then a 16-year-old child was born. Because the stasis fluid contained concentrated data about contemporary culture and theoretical knowledge, she was born as a genius, but she has no lived experience.
ASHLEY: Is Saylor… a mother now?
CHARLOTTE: I mean, terminal took her DNA sample and the machine generated a new DNA based on that. So whether any one of us admits it or not, Stelle is Saylor’s biological descendant.
STELLE: Everything Auntie said is true.
CHARLOTTE: (bows) Charlotte for TED Talks, thanks for listening.
ASHLEY: Auntie?
STELLE: TED Talks?
CHARLOTTE: I hate it when I answer one question and it creates only more questions…
SAYLOR: (chuckles) Welcome to my world.
STELLE: Mom, no one was coming, and I was sleeping in my room.
SAYLOR: You’re doing good!
CHARLOTTE: Should I bring some diapers?

Ashley nudges Charlotte with her elbow.

SAYLOR: How are you managing your… orthostatic hypotension?
ASHLEY: (quietly) What?
CHARLOTTE: (quietly) Headrush.
STELLE: Much better. At least I don’t wake up our neighbors when I get up.
ASHLEY: “Our neighbors?”
STELLE: I live here.
ASHLEY: Oh my gosh. My townmate is a genetically engineered genius!
SAYLOR: Enjoy it. She’s a fun time.
ASHLEY: Trouble?
SAYLOR: (yawns) Well, I would rather prefer to have more hours of sleep.
STELLE: (side-hugs Saylor and Ashley) No, not right now. Let’s go stretching our legs.
CHARLOTTE: I was waiting for it for ages!
STELLE: (to Charlotte) But no teleporting. Is your parasol primed?
CHARLOTTE: (opens her parasol) Locked and loaded. 62% charge.
STELLE: (presses the parasol button to close it) Keep it until we need it.
CHARLOTTE: I’ll need a power plant to power it up fully…
SAYLOR: Don’t. I’m not watching you turning into ash.
ASHLEY: Ahem.
SAYLOR: Sorry. Bad joke.
CHARLOTTE: Actually, my high-voltage needs are not necessary.

Charlotte pulls a rectangular plug out of her umbrella shaft to reveal the USB port.

ASHLEY: USB 3?
CHARLOTTE: USB 4. Compatible with USB 2 and 3 anyway. And Thunderbolt 5—you know me, say “thunder” and I’ll buy it. This parasol takes approximately 10 hours to charge completely from 0% to 100% with 100W.
STELLE: Sounds like a very simple math to me.
ASHLEY: So every percent is precious, right?
CHARLOTTE: Absolutely. So if we go to some late-night cafe, I’m definitely about to use even USB 3 to squeeze out a couple of percents more.
SAYLOR: Well, it saves you from the quest of catching the thunderstorm. You simply put your thingamajig onto the charging deck, and in a few days you have enough power to travel anywhere. Convenience.
CHARLOTTE: Uh-huh. Convenience. USB is a bit of a bottleneck in your era.
STELLE: Oh stop it, it’s universal!
SAYLOR: Leave your parasol to me tomorrow and I’ll solder on a high-power charging port.
CHARLOTTE: We’ll do that both.
SAYLOR: Deal.
STELLE: And I’ll control the process and maybe help optimize it.
ASHLEY: I wouldn’t mind watching it if I had some free time.
