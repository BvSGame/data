# Mad Auntie

A script draft for _Bombs vs. Sleepwalkers_. Written in **July 23, 2026** by **liledix⁴**. This is a follow-up to _Headcrash_.

----

2024, Saylor's rental. Evening. The front door is open, because Saylor expects just any of her friends to come. There’s a custom-built security system that detects unfamiliar intruders. So far, there are none. Saylor sits at the living room, on a couch, checking her tablet.

SAYLOR: Hee-hee, sleepwalkers will be so welcomed by this new gadgetry. Mmmm, flawless.

Eventually, Charlotte enters. 22-year-old girl in her casual modern attire, her Victorian curls remain on her blonde hair.

CHARLOTTE: Oh, sunshine! There you are!
SAYLOR: Hi Char.
CHARLOTTE: Come on. Where’s the excited face of my ever energetic girl?
SAYLOR: I’m tired. Well, a tiny bit tired. Almost not tired. Totally forgot what tired is.
CHARLOTTE: Uh-huh. I don’t buy that. Do you know what I really wanna buy? A drink! A drink for us two in the 2047 club! Remember I know a few places where you’re not considered a teen?
SAYLOR: Ah, your favorite 2047. The time where you had some warehouse with what, 15 computers to perform your coding work?
CHARLOTTE: We don’t talk about computers now, okay? Delete computers from your mind and fill the space with the only word: fun!
SAYLOR: Charley.
CHARLOTTE: Oh blooming heck. Blooming wreck! What now?
SAYLOR: I’m not clubbing.
CHARLOTTE: May I ask who you are and what have you done to my Saylor, sunshine?
SAYLOR: I... Sigh... I have a daughter.
CHARLOTTE: Since when? Wait... Let me look at you… Your belly is flat. Is it recent? You might be confusing gender, maybe to troll me—
SAYLOR: This is true.
CHARLOTTE: Uh… (checking her forearm device) My PTT is functioning properly. I followed the sequence correctly. Please don’t gaslight me.
STELLE: Mom?

Stelle enters the room. Same green sundress, same brown wavy long hair, same style, no change.

STELLE: Oh. Good evening.
CHARLOTTE: Yeah. Hi. (whispering to Saylor) Saylor, we have a security breach. A teenager-shaped security breach.
SAYLOR: This security breach has a name. And the name is Stelle. My daughter. One and only.
STELLE: It’s me. Uh, mom, I took a walk down the street safely.
SAYLOR: I know, sweetie, I’ve tracked your GPS. You might buy something, I’ve figured out a virtual bank card with shared budget.
CHARLOTTE: Saylor. I have questions.
SAYLOR: Shoot.

Charlotte stands up and slowly walks around Stelle.

CHARLOTTE: So. She’s from the future, right?
SAYLOR: Yes.
CHARLOTTE: Your _own_ future.
SAYLOR: No.
CHARLOTTE: Artificial.
SAYLOR: Completely right.
CHARLOTTE: She was a teen like that or you waited for her to grow?
SAYLOR: Former.
CHARLOTTE: You mad girl.
SAYLOR: Who, me?
CHARLOTTE: Yes, you. You’re utterly bonkers. Bananas. Wicked. The list goes on.
SAYLOR: Don’t talk to me like that!
CHARLOTTE: I can! And I will. Because you know what?
SAYLOR: What?

Charlotte stops, wraps her arm around Stelle, and looks at Saylor.

CHARLOTTE: Welcome to motherhood.
SAYLOR: What?
CHARLOTTE: I know the deal, I have three kids.
SAYLOR: What?!
CHARLOTTE: All generated. Scattered across time. And I’ll never introduce them to you because it’s my personal stuff.
SAYLOR: Look at you go, you big mother.
CHARLOTTE: Two of them are adults anyway, they moved on.
STELLE: Age acceleration?
CHARLOTTE: One of them cheated. Another one was banned from it. Fool me once, but not twice. I threatened them with a one-way ticket to the exploration of the Sun. (reaches out her hand to Stelle) I’m Charley, by the way.
STELLE: Stelle. Nice to meet you.

They shake hands.

SAYLOR: It’s Charlotte.
CHARLOTTE: I know my own name, thank you very much! (to Stelle) For you, sweetheart, it's Mad Auntie Cherry. Don’t make your mommy make the rules.
STELLE: So you have experience. Maternal experience.
CHARLOTTE: Oh look at your cutesy eyes brightening. Shame you weren’t introduced earlier. It’s mothers time.

Chatlotte frees Stelle and walks to Saylor.

SAYLOR: I’m going nowhere. This kid can break the law and not even realize it! She needs guidance!
CHARLOTTE: (tapping some sequence on her PTT) She’s not an idiot! She surely knows how to cross her own fingers. (to Stelle) You take some rest from your mom, alright? I’m stealing her.
SAYLOR: You don’t!
CHARLOTTE: I’m not being polite now! No belts to fasten! (finishes with PTT, grabs Saylor’s arm) Zap!!
SAYLOR: You chaotic—

Flash! Bang! Charlotte and Saylor disappear.

STELLE: Hey! Where have you gone?

Silence.

STELLE: Not the… temporal teleportation.

Stelle sits down.

STELLE: (holding her forearm) I need that device.
