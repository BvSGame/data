# The Unsleeping Agent

A script draft for _Bombs vs. Sleepwalkers_. Written in **July 19, 2026** by **liledix⁴**. This is a follow-up to _A Delicate Trouble_. Started as a part of _Slices of Time_ series, the script size has ballooned beyond 500 words quite quickly.

----

2038, the resistance headquarters.  1 AM.  SCARLETT is sitting at the table, working on the motorcycle’s engine, while the motorcycle itself stands nearby, partially disassembled.  She wears tank top and leggings, and her blonde hair is tied back in a slightly messy high ponytail instead of the usual loose style.  Just for a moment, she switches to a paper folder.  She opens it, sees a single sheet of paper, takes it out, and takes a serious long look.  The paper contains only one word, “The.”

SCARLETT
(to herself, snorting)
That would unsettle her.

SCARLETT puts the paper back into the folder and slides it away.  She proceeds to work on the engine.

MARIE enters the room, her classic, corporate look is unchanged.

MARIE
Well, well, well.  If it isn’t my _colleague_ doing an extra work off-hours just to spite me.

SCARLETT
Sounds more like your M.O., sweetheart.  You have too high opinion of yourself.

MARIE
When you’re around, it figures.

MARIE sits down across from SCARLETT.

MARIE
Thanks to Saylor and her SUV for bringing me to this wonderful sight.

SCARLETT
Yeah.  Thanks to Saylor for providing me a decent toolkit.

MARIE
What are you doing anyway?  Planning deception?  Is this something to fool me tomorrow?  Or outperform me while having some kickstart ready to deploy?  Obviously perfecting your efficiency for the legwork.  You always want to be the first, even before the day starts.

SCARLETT
(sighs)
Marie, you’re clinically obsessed.  Not even analytical, but obsessed.  Analysis implies that you use the information to advance in the mission.  Obsession implies that you have views on me.  At this rate, I begin to think Rick won peaceful sleep for once.

MARIE
(blushing slightly)
I--  N-nonsense!  Me?  Views?  Personal?!  I’ll--  I’ll file an official complaint!

SCARLETT
(chuckles melodically)
I’ll use this shock tactics on duty, just to keep your eyes peeled.  Thanks for the suggestion!

MARIE
You don’t, you sly one.  You’ll lead both of us to the definitive failure if you do this.

SCARLETT
I know.  You can’t see I’m joking at this moment.  I can allow myself a little jest when I’m out of commission.
(points at hibernating LISA)
Even androids have hibernation cycles.  She’s not busy calculating the room 24/7 to apply her “Human Plus” branding to find out if she can cook without poisoning us or tidy up without scratching the floor.  Well, she fakes her 8-hour sleep to pass for human, but she’s committed to the bit for sure.

MARIE
Why aren’t _you_ sleeping?

SCARLETT
I was trying to get your attention.  And, I obviously succeeded.  Say, what do you think of Stelle?

MARIE
Saylor’s fake child?  What should I think of her?

SCARLETT
She’s wonderful.  You could be too if you listened to what I’m saying.

MARIE
You don’t--  You don’t compare me to a genetically engineered girl who hadn’t seen enough days with her own eyes but takes everyone as an authority figure.

SCARLETT
Don’t deny it, she’s good.

MARIE
That’s not how Scarlett speaks!  Where’s your bossy demeanor?

SCARLETT
Marie.  Again.  I’m off-duty.  Give me a break.

MARIE
Agents are never off-duty!  Especially in 2038.

SCARLETT
Oh yeah?  Is that why you’re chatting with me now instead of doing some useful research?

MARIE
This is a research considering inner circle.

SCARLETT
Mm-hm.  You’re trying to test the waters to see if I’m a match for you.

MARIE
Professionally.  Only professionally.

At last, MARIE takes the folder and opens it.

SCARLETT
I can see that.

MARIE
(looking at the paper)
Huh.
(closes the folder)
That was intentional.  You want to fool me.
(quieter but more intense)
What are you hiding?

SCARLETT
(pauses)
Me?  Hm.  Marie, you’re a fool indeed.  Even a toddler would understand.
(grabbing empty bottle of bubblegum soda Saylor finished hours ago)
I’m transparent as the glass.

MARIE
Say it in your own words.

SCARLETT
Marie.  Drop the act and try to be human.  That’s all I’m asking.  Don’t make me think that your 2024 agency swallowed you alive and you forgot how to live a normal life.  Life doesn’t consist only of Excel, world is not made for calculations.  Get a grip.

MARIE
You can’t be so easy.  No, you aren’t.

SCARLETT
The.

MARIE
Excuse me?

SCARLETT
I said, the.

MARIE
Sigh…  If you think I won’t find out what you hide, you think too low of me.

SCARLETT
(leaning forward slightly)
Do it.

MARIE
Are you challenging me?

SCARLETT
Absolutely.  Wanna get drowned in my personality?  I couldn’t be more honored.  But as soon as you’ve picked me apart so thoroughly that there’s not a single secret left unrevealed, not a single bone left unturned, not a single corner of the soul left unscraped, I’ll reserve the right to demand that you learn to live like a normal person.  Otherwise, I’ll take your personal involvement as a grave insult.  Is that understood?

MARIE
Gosh.  You really _are_ committed.

SCARLETT
I’m capable of mixing threatening and domestic.  Don’t underestimate me, please.  If you can’t handle it, don’t be in my company.

MARIE
Hm.

MARIE gets up from the table.  She walks around the table, passing by SCARLETT, who is still sitting there, but MARIE lightly taps SCARLETT’s shoulders with her fingers.

MARIE
Scarlett, Scarlett, Scarlett…

Then MARIE slowly returns to her seat and slowly sits down.  MARIE takes a second of silence, clicks with her mouth, then says quietly.

MARIE
Challenge accepted.

SCARLETT
I knew you wouldn’t resist it.

MARIE
A biggest walking mystery box?  My colleague from the future who is sabotaging the agency to stand with the opposition?  Please.
