# Like Mother, Like Daughter: Part 1

A script draft for _Bombs vs. Sleepwalkers_. Written in **July 23–24, 2026** by **liledix⁴**. Edited in **July 27, 2026**.

----

3000s. Evening. Some large dark room, something industrial-looking.

STELLE: Mom, can you tell me exactly why we’re here? And wherever you go to the future, why is it always dark? I prefer places with some mood lighting.
SAYLOR: O ye of little faith…
STELLE: Bible?
SAYLOR: TV shows. Or popular quotes subreddit, I don’t remember. Well. Alice gave me some useful info. She discovered this neatly hidden place mentioned in a databank of the sleepwalker she destroyed.
STELLE: Where are we?
SAYLOR: A sleepwalker warehouse.

Slowly zooming out to a view of rows of inactive sleepwalker robots standing upright, waiting for their moment.

Then, back to our girls, walking through these rows.

STELLE: Right in the belly of the beast…
SAYLOR: Yeah, this beast is about to have some indigestion. We must demolish it, but shhh, very quiet. Watch your step.

Now they whisper as they walk.

STELLE: Okay. I get it. Calm before the storm.
SAYLOR: Exactly.
STELLE: What are you gonna do? Do you have a plan?
SAYLOR: Lisa.
STELLE: (checks her smartwatch) She’s offline.
SAYLOR: And emergency channel?
STELLE: “Do Not Disturb” mode.
SAYLOR: Stellar… Probably busy playing chess in 2038 with Scarlett at 10 PM.
STELLE: Bored girl finds more ways to put her Human Plus features into practice.
SAYLOR: Too busy for real missions. (checks the tablet and _her_ smartwatch, they’re synchronizing) But, I have some info cached. It’s an offline map. We must go… (points in an alternative direction) …there. Up 20 feet via a vertical ladder.
STELLE: Mom. I don’t know if my body handles heights.
SAYLOR: Try to go up without looking down.
STELLE: But how are we going to go down?
SAYLOR: We’ll jump.
STELLE: Mom.
SAYLOR: Let’s hope for an elevator. Or a soft landing.

Stelle shakes her head disapprovingly. However, they proceed.

STELLE: So Alice then.
SAYLOR: She’s an inhabitant of this time period. She spends too much time on the streets, our 19-year-old self-proclaimed “demolitions expert.”
STELLE: Oh so she knows explosives.
SAYLOR: How to use? Yes. Why do they boom? No. In your case, it would be the other way round.
STELLE: And she has the same hair as me? Long and brown?
SAYLOR: But not wavy. She doesn’t care about her hair. She cares about making something boom. This lightens her day.
STELLE: And with this description, you expect her to be helpful.
SAYLOR: She roamed enough areas, but not this. And this is clearly sparking some interest.
STELLE: Can she ever be serious?
SAYLOR: Well, normally she’s a dork.
STELLE: And you listened to her.
SAYLOR: She was convincing.
STELLE: Mm-hm.
SAYLOR: Well. It makes sense, isn’t it? A dork sends us to the dark.
STELLE: Not really, Mom. It’s a fallacy.
SAYLOR: Not really, girl. It’s a joke. (whispering) Play along.
????: (echoing) Three.
SAYLOR: (to Stelle) What do you mean?
STELLE: What? I said nothing.
????: (echoing) One.
SAYLOR: What are you counting?
STELLE: No, listen. Listen closely.
????: (echoing) Four.
SAYLOR: Three… One… Four…
STELLE: Yes. A number.
SAYLOR: Pi.
????: (echoing) Two.
STELLE: (correcting the wrong next digit) One!!
SAYLOR: (spotting the interrupted sequence) Shhhhh! That person isn’t counting pi!
STELLE: A trap.
SAYLOR: Move. Move-move-move.

They quicken their pace, but at the turn they come face to face with another figure hidden in the darkness. However, even in the dark, the facial features are very distinct.

DG-STELLE #2: Five.
SAYLOR: (abruptly exhaling) Oh my!
DG-STELLE #1: (echoing behind them) Nine.
STELLE: I swear, I don’t have clones!
SAYLOR: How will you explain her?
STELLE: A robot!
SAYLOR: That’s lowkey racist of you.

They walk around the person they bumped into, but they notice… an army walking in the distance. Their silhouettes are recognizable.

DG-STELLES: (randomly, with varying intonations) Two. Six. Five. Three. Five. Eight. Nine. Seven. Nine. Three. Two.
SAYLOR: Tell me, Stelle, is this _my_ nightmare or _yours_?
STELLE: I had no nightmares in my life.
SAYLOR: I guess you’ll experience it live. Or rather, _we_ will. What a privilege.

They run away.

SAYLOR: Okay, we stand no chance in hide-and-seek, right?
STELLE: Oh I don’t know. I think I could blend in. If you weren’t here standing out.
SAYLOR: I don’t do green sundresses.
STELLE: We could split up!
SAYLOR: Uh-huh, and I’ll be in a greater trouble, that’s so tactical.

As they run, another doppelganger of Stelle reaches out from around the corner and grabs Stelle’s arm. Saylor stops to help Stelle.

SAYLOR: You don’t!
STELLE: Let go, you!
DG-STELLES: (randomly, with varying intonations, echoing) Three. Eight. Four. Six. Two. Six. Four. Three. Three.

Saylor slaps the doppelganger’s arm. Stelle is free. They keep running down the corridors.

STELLE: Do you realize you kind of slapped me?
SAYLOR: No, I didn’t.
STELLE: That was my hand.
SAYLOR: Don’t make it weird. Your hand can’t hold onto your other hand to stop you from running. Use your head.
STELLE: Okay, not my hand, my alter ego’s, but the point still stands.
SAYLOR: Why are you projecting your psychological feelings on your double’s physical feelings?
STELLE: That hand was warm.
SAYLOR: And… soft?
STELLE: Not a robot…
SAYLOR: They’re…
STELLE: Not robots.
SAYLOR: But they’re substantial!
STELLE: Clones?
SAYLOR: I don’t buy that!
STELLE: A turn, mom. No straight way.
SAYLOR: Please no cornering, please no cornering…

Saylor and Stelle run around the corner and are met by another crowd of Stelles.

SAYLOR: (loudly) Damn!

They run back to the lone doppelgänger of Stelle. They dodge her. As they keep running, they see a previous horde of Stelles, but they don’t stop running toward them. As they got somewhat close, they run into the turn and stop to catch their breath.

STELLE: We can’t even use our map to navigate! Admit it, Mom, we failed!
DG-STELLES: (collectively as they approach) Nine. Eight. Seven. Six. Five.

Saylor is noticeably terrified. An army of Stelle doppelgängers shows up.

DG-STELLES: Four. Three. Two. One.
DG-STELLE: (singular) Zero.

One of the doppelgängers walks close. Saylor and Stelle don’t step back.

STELLE: I was right. They’re organic.
DG-STELLE: You.
STELLE: Me?
SAYLOR: Uh… You have an extra one here. Can I leave?
DG-STELLE: Stand. (pointing at Stelle) Explain.
SAYLOR: What, explain why you’re a bunch of fakes? What do you want to hear?
DG-STELLE: How?
STELLE: Explain your question.
DG-STELLE: How is this possible?
SAYLOR: Look, I’m just Sa— uh, a technical student, and this is a… human with an exceptional theoretical knowledge, but we’re not scientists! We’re not biologists. Just passing through. Basically, I’m just here for delivering technobabble. Like a proud taxi driver.
DG-STELLE: Saylor.
SAYLOR: Namedropper! (to Stelle) Look, girl, we have a namedropper! Someone, arrest her!
DG-STELLE: Saylor the Mother.
ALL DG-STELLES: Saylor the Mother.
SAYLOR: Oh no… The news are spreading fast like a plague… Stellar! This is my tagline now, isn’t it? What now, I have a thousand of daughters? Who are you, anyway? What are you?
DG-STELLE: Echoes.
SAYLOR: We’ve clearly heard that the first time.
DG-STELLE: Echoes of time.
DG-STELLE #2: Just like you, Stelle. You’re an echo.
STELLE: What?
DG-STELLE #2: Like mother.
DG-SAYLOR: (from behind) Like daughter.

Saylor and Stelle turn around and see an army or Saylors. All having identical outfits with denim overalls.

STELLE: Whoa…
SAYLOR: I’m officially in hell… (to Stelle) Honey. I think I need to reconsider my outfit. Girls, my denim will be… green.

Saylor grabs Stelle abruptly and quickly drags her along to hide both of them behind a secret wall she spotted a minute ago. Now they’re whispering, leaning against the wall and glancing around from time to time.

STELLE: Bad time for quips, Mom.
SAYLOR: No, I mean it. Look at them cosplaying me. I’m so viral. That’s a lot of dedication and cotton wasted. This is not the sight I lived my 20 years for. I’m just Saylor!
STELLE: I can agree with you. It’s not a pleasant experience to see yourself multiplied on your third day of living. I don’t feel unique anymore.
SAYLOR: Funny you say that.
STELLE: I’m being objective.
SAYLOR: Speaking of objectives. Mind some cardio?
STELLE: Run.

Saylor nods. They run a short distance down the secret route. Saylor stops Stelle and looks around to make sure it’s safe. 

SAYLOR: Look. (pulls out the tablet) We’re closer to our destination. Now listen. Out there, I was playing dumb deliberately. Who knows if we were surveiled? We couldn’t spill them all the beans. I waited for the right moment.
STELLE: You tactician. (punches Saylor’s arm) You scared me to death!
SAYLOR: Now… We’re here to shut down one of the systems Darkrock planted here in the 3000s. Plus, I intend to find out why exactly he’s doing it.
STELLE: And the clones? Echoes? Whatever they are.
SAYLOR: They weren’t on my menu today. We’ll deal with them later. Stick a pin in that.
STELLE: (takes tablet from Saylor’s hand) Well, come along.
