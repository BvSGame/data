# Charlotte Adventures: Part 5 (Unfinished)

A script draft for _Bombs vs. Sleepwalkers_. Written in **September 14, 2026** by **liledix⁴**.

----

2038, abandoned house. Late night.

RICK and CHARLOTTE are slowly walking, shining their flashlights from one corner to another.

RICK: So, the house that contains some well-hidden files of Darkrock. I wouldn’t say it’s necessarily a decent place for hiding anything.
CHARLOTTE: It’s called “hiding in plain sight,” Ricky. Only a complete fool would start looking for clues at the abandoned old house.
RICK: You think that discovery would make some dent in Darkrock’s reputation as the President of Earth?
CHARLOTTE: The man is not indestructible. There must be something that could overthrow him. That tiny tasty clue he’s afraid of getting revealed. And Lisa smells we’re in the right place.
RICK: Lisa can’t smell anything. Instead, she would say: “No olfactory system was implanted into my synthetic body.”
CHARLOTTE: Would she?
RICK: She did the same comment about salivary glands.
CHARLOTTE: Rick. She can’t talk about salivary glands in a vacuum. How did you prompt this response?
RICK: I just used an idiom “spit it out.” As in “tell it as it is.”
CHARLOTTE: Okay, natural charmer, I’ll take this point.

Suddenly, CHARLOTTE pauses, then he stops RICK with her arm outstretched.

CHARLOTTE: (terrified) Rick…
RICK: What is it? What have you noticed?
CHARLOTTE: Look down…

They notice someone lying on the floor faced down. Body looks suspiciously familiar.

RICK: That’s… That’s not possible. Char, there is a possibility that there’s another similarly looking person.
CHARLOTTE: Physique is way too similar. You can’t convince me this is _someone else entirely_.

RICK and CHARLOTTE are leaning closer to the lying body. They turn the head upwards. It’s another CHARLOTTE, unconscious.

RICK: No…
CHARLOTTE: Okay… If this is… me… I better be sleeping.
RICK: She doesn’t breathe. At all.
CHARLOTTE: Her PTT…

CHARLOTTE takes a closer look at the forearm. PTT of the unconscious CHARLOTTE is…

CHARLOTTE: It’s scorched. Damaged.
RICK: What could happen to her?
CHARLOTTE: I hate to know. Try turning her body all the way upward.

Unconscious CHARLOTTE is being turned upwards. Clothing in the abdominal area is scorched too. Badly scorched.

CHARLOTTE: (quiet hyperventilating) No-no-no-no-no…
RICK: Is she…?
CHARLOTTE: (nervous) Not killed. I refuse. Do you hear me? I refuse to take this as an argument! I **wasn’t** killed!!
RICK: Okay, okay, calm down. Collect yourself. You’re still alive. Just think what could really happen.
CHARLOTTE: This body must be fake, okay? Fake! It must be a clever trick of mine. Maybe I faked my death to escape a terrible outcome. A failed mission. I don’t know! I really don’t know, don’t pressure me!!

CHARLOTTE cries. RICK hugs her. For a few seconds, it’s quiet, just CHARLOTTE crying on RICK’s shoulder.

CHARLOTTE: I can’t be dead.
RICK: Sh-h-h. You’re not. Listen to me, please. This is the house where _something_ has happened. We don’t know what it is, and we mustn’t believe everything at first glance. We mustn’t make conclusions. Please, let’s just investigate.
CHARLOTTE: I don’t think I can unsee this. Do you understand? As we investigate, I have to keep the image of dead self in my mind. Is this sane?
RICK: Charlotte. You’ve seen the most insane stuff in your life. Is this really where you draw the line?
CHARLOTTE: That’s new… Totally unexpected. Absolutely haunting. But hey. Please, if I won’t be behaving confidently, don’t judge me. This is a kind of situation I’m not willing to be in. But looks like it’s personal now. And I hate it.
RICK: Just rely on me as we go through.
CHARLOTTE: Right. Yeah, right. To the mission then. I hope we’ll figure this out. I don’t think it can be _this_ simple. I reject this reality.
RICK: Moving on.

The investigation continues.
