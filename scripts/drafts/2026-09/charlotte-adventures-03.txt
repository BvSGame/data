# Charlotte Adventures: Part 3 (Snippet)

A script draft for _Bombs vs. Sleepwalkers_. Written in **September 6, 2026** by **liledix⁴**.

----

2024, RICK’s rental apartment. 3 AM. RICK is sleeping, but occasionally you can see him slightly twitching.

From RICK’s current dream: 1838, London palace. Hallway. From the actual memories, the first day of RICK and SAYLOR appearing in the scene.

The time-traveling trio—SAYLOR, RICK, AND CHARLOTTE, all dressed up in era-appropriate outfits—is standing off to the side, heatedly discussing their positioning in the situation.

SAYLOR: Rick? Are you sure you hid my handbag with the tablet safely?
RICK: Don’t worry, it’s in my guest room, and it’s properly locked.
SAYLOR: Good. (taps her smartwatch designed to look like a Victorian wristwatch) I still operate it remotely. Well, while connectivity isn’t failing me. Bluetooth range is weak, and Wi-Fi hotspot I’ve created is unstable. This watch needs better wireless modules, honestly…
CHARLOTTE: I may take care of fetching it.
SAYLOR: It’s not urgent.
RICK: Right. Cover story time. What names do we take this time?
SAYLOR: I’m considering: why bother if we can go with Saylorine?
RICK: Saylor. Adding “-ine” won’t fool anyone. Besides, it sounds like a creepy monster lurking in the shadows. I’m not calling you this. Be more creative.
CHARLOTTE: Sunshine, everyone knows you’re Gentleman Rick here. You won’t need to cover.
RICK: Great… Yet another point in time where my genuine self is known and popular. Char, you’re a dangerous company.
CHARLOTTE: To be fair, they knew real me for years now. Well, sans time travel shenanigans and high-tech balderdash. Sorry for the reflex. It wasn’t recent.
RICK: Anyways… Just one rare century where I don’t have to pretend… Saylor? You?
SAYLOR: Call me Lady Catherine. Charlie, is there anyone with this name?
CHARLOTTE: We already have two ladies with this name.
SAYLOR: Oh! I should meet them. Catherine Trio! Imagine that. (to RICK) Ca-three-rine?
RICK quietly facepalms.
CHARLOTTE: I can arrange it.
SAYLOR: Now?
CHARLOTTE: Why not? Time waits for no traveler. Let’s start our palace wanderings with this meeting!
SAYLOR: Stellar!
CHARLOTTE: And. Gentleman Rick? I’d like to ask you to dance at the next ball. Which takes place this evening, 7 o’clock.
SAYLOR: Oh my goodness! Look at her, Rick. We’ve just arrived and she’s already picking out dance partners. What a hurricane.
RICK: Victorian Storm, actually.
CHARLOTTE: Yes.
SAYLOR: I know, I know, but “hurricane” has its flair as well, don’t you think? Hurricane is something fast and sweeping, and storm is something that comes slowly but strikes when you expect it the least.
CHARLOTTE: I can be both.
RICK: Charley, I can’t dance.
CHARLOTTE: I don’t want you to have this experience in advance. I want you to take part in the chaos. Come on, you can’t refuse this exceptional fun!
SAYLOR: (leaning in) Honestly, my dear brother? You would look foolish refusing this.
RICK: I accept your invitation.
SAYLOR: Seriously? So quick?
RICK: It’s Charlotte. What’s gonna happen?
SAYLOR: I don’t know. The way she looks, she suggests that by the end, your bones be thrown to feed the high-society wolves.
CHARLOTTE: Blooming heck, Saylor, that’s mental! Don’t worry, Rick, she’s dramatizing. Shapeshifters are only a myth. I’m offering an unforgettable social experience. Besides, Saylie will dance as well.
SAYLOR: Absolutely not! Now we draw the line in the… marble floor.
CHARLOTTE: So what, you’re going to play jealous statue while I’m spinning your bestie around?
SAYLOR: Heels, Charlotte! I can’t do heels! Do you want me _that_ hard to appear the most miserable person in the room?! Are you really dying to see me sweeping the floor with my dress while I’m lying down?!
CHARLOTTE: Heeled footwear is not mandatory. It just adds to the authenticity and grace. But for the sake of safety, we have other options.
SAYLOR: Thank goodness. For a second I was worried I was left behind. By an entire century that is roughly 170 years before my birth. What a privilege.
CHARLOTTE: (puts her arm around SAYLOR’s shoulders) Oh Saylie-Bee, sweetheart, I would never! I wish my friends the best, and I’ll make sure you’re treated exceptionally well. I don’t want you to feel alien in my homeland era.
SAYLOR: Char. You’re unbearably comforting. When you do this, I feel like I’m physically melting, and I mean it. Can you take a look? Is my face still in one piece? Is my cheek slipping down?
CHARLOTTE: No, love. You’re doing stellar.
SAYLOR: Stop this flattery, I can’t.
CHARLOTTE: (raises her free hand suggestively) Rick. Joint hug?
SAYLOR: Yes, save me, I shouldn’t suffer in embrace alone!
RICK: (smiles, joins the side hug) As if I would ever turn it down.
CHARLOTTE: Right, sunshines. Gosh, I almost never say this in the plural… Let’s bring some supernova into this corner of society.
SAYLOR: Supernova? You’re teasing me to say the thing, aren’t you?
RICK: Well, say it.
SAYLOR: Stellar! Let’s get dazzling!
CHARLOTTE: That’s the spirit, I love you for this, come on!

And the laughing trio goes down the hallway.

The dream ends.

Change of the scene. Same 2024, same 3 AM, but it’s SAYLOR’s rental house. SAYLOR’s bedroom.

SAYLOR sleeps peacefully. Unlike RICK, she’s not twitching.

Again, we see the dream that reflects the memory of actual events.

1838, London palace. SAYLOR’s guest room. SAYLOR just sits at the table and closely analyzes the spare Victorian dress. She doesn’t miss a slightest detail. Someone knocks on the door.

SAYLOR: Enter!

VIOLET enters the room and closes the door.

VIOLET: At last. We have a conversation.
SAYLOR: Sorry, Violet. My scheduling is a mess. I’m the least punctual person you will ever meet in your life. Just deal with it.
VIOLET: You’re aware of my agreement with Rick.
SAYLOR: He told me. He’s a good brother.
VIOLET: Do you remember about _our_ agreement?
SAYLOR: Yeah. The exact same “Gal, I want you to fit in, so the dukes and duchesses wouldn’t ever raise an eyebrow.” Except you miss one core point.
VIOLET: May I hear it?
SAYLOR: I’m not present in your society. And I’m glad. Poor Rick, Charlotte wanted him to feel seen, and instead he’s on his way to become a Victorian superstar. Dude hasn’t even said yes.
VIOLET: Do you realize that I’m reading through the subtext without understanding the words you’re throwing at me? You may be a foreigner who pays us a visit for a couple of hours a week, but you still speak English.
SAYLOR: I have no intention to filter myself in my own guest room while the only audience I have is a lady who humbly listened to my most crude remarks imaginable. Literally read the room.
VIOLET: So you refuse to be a part of our society.
SAYLOR: Violet. This is the most honest you’ll hear from me. I’m here as a protector. I’m protecting Rick, I’m protecting Charlotte, and I’m protecting your sweet night sleep from the clockwork men. The very army of men your silly gentlemen can’t take down. At this point, I’m really stronger than any man you can name. Where I’m present, mechanical threats go boom. And you know that. If you value this service, don’t expect more from me. I won’t participate in your tearjerking melodrama.
VIOLET: So you’re leaving Rick to handle all social matters in your humble circle.
SAYLOR: You already took him against his will. You can’t do the same with me.
VIOLET: Understood.
SAYLOR: But hey. I don’t want to lose you just because I don’t fancy manners.
VIOLET: Who said I’m offended?
SAYLOR: Till the next time, then?
VIOLET: Yes, Saylor. See you later.
SAYLOR: So you used my actual name… Heh. I shouldn’t have been telling you that.
VIOLET: Your secret will be buried with me, I guarantee that. And, I respect your viewpoint. Even if I fiercely disagree with it.

VIOLET leaves the room. SAYLOR continues analyzing the dress.

The dream ends.

But we’re still staying in 2024. Same SAYLOR’s rental house, but the next room. CHARLOTTE is spending the night in the guest room next door. She’s dreaming of…

2345, the high-technology streets.

Flash! Bang! CHARLOTTE and LISA materialized.

CHARLOTTE: Phew! So, my “extended human” friend. Where are we going now?
