# Charlotte Adventures: Part 1

A script draft for _Bombs vs. Sleepwalkers_. Written in **September 1–5, 2026** by **liledix⁴**.

----

2024, RICK’s rental flat. Bedroom. 2 AM.

RICK is sleeping. Knock-knock on the window. RICK doesn’t react.

Knock-knock gets harder. RICK slowly opens the eyes. As soon as he notices who’s knocking…

RICK: A-a-ah!

Outside the window, against the backdrop of the town at night, CHARLOTTE is hanging from a rope tied to the roof of an apartment building. Wearing white shirt (with top button undone) and black trousers, her blonde hair with curly ends remain constant. Folded multi-functional umbrella is attached to her side.

CHARLOTTE: (muffled, unclear) Open up!!

RICK sighs, gets up from bed, and walks over to the window. He opens it.

CHARLOTTE: (waving her free hand, but instantly grips the rope tighter with that hand) Hello.
RICK: Charley. As a time traveler, you must understand what time it is.
CHARLOTTE: Exactly. But you miss one thing.
RICK: Which is?
CHARLOTTE: I have my own daily routine.
RICK: Which hangs by a thread over the abyss.
CHARLOTTE: (looks up) Untimely pun. (looks at RICK) Any chance for a cuppa?
RICK: Surely you considered a backup plan. Or, an extra length of the rope.
CHARLOTTE: Yes. Fall free and think later.
RICK: (tired, turns toward the exit door) Come in, wacky adventurer.
CHARLOTTE: Hey! You might give a lady a hand!
RICK: Swing over.
CHARLOTTE: Right. “Do it yourself,” then. I see you, Ricky. It’s a test. You’re tempting me to pull some stunts.

CHARLOTTE swings and jumps through the window into the room. She does a somersault, sits down on one knee, and throws her arms in the air.

CHARLOTTE: Ta-da-a!
RICK: Sh-h-h!!
CHARLOTTE: (stands up) Okay. One nice cuppa, and we go to the mission.
RICK: Mission?! Char, it could wait!
CHARLOTTE: I don’t take “no” for an answer. You know me.
RICK: Sigh… Fine. But let’s draw the line. After that mission, we take a break.
CHARLOTTE: Uh, I was about to suggest that anyway. You just said it earlier.

Kitchen. CHARLOTTE sips tea.

RICK: Any good?
CHARLOTTE: Hmm?
RICK: Normally you don’t drink tea without leaving a review.
CHARLOTTE: Ah. The tea was an excuse. (unfolds PTT to reveal touchscreen) Take a look at what we have on hand.
RICK: Uh-huh… That warehouse… But why now? Why can’t we wait?
CHARLOTTE: Look at the datamining. Lisa discovered that there’s a surprise attack scheduled for 8 AM. In 2024. Today.
RICK: Where?
CHARLOTTE: Saylor’s rental house. We must act now.
RICK: Why couldn’t you do it earlier?
CHARLOTTE: Blooming heck, Rick! I’ve got this info an hour ago! Lisa sent me this after midnight. Do you really expect me to procrastinate so casually?
RICK: I’m really gonna have a very serious, unfriendly conversation with Lisa once it’s over.
CHARLOTTE: Poor Saylie wasn’t happy to take a look either, but even she verified it while being busy building an experimental robot model in her garage.
RICK: I get your point. But why _me_?
CHARLOTTE: Use your head. Who else is there?
RICK: Scarl— No, she’s following strict schedule… Toni? No, this task is not for her specialty… Alice? She won’t comprehend the situation…
CHARLOTTE: See? Even you’re struggling.

RICK’s phone rings.

RICK: Uh… Hang on. (pulls out the phone) It’s Saylor.
CHARLOTTE: Just in time.
RICK: (answers the phone) Yes?
SAYLOR (VO): Rick. Have you met Charley?
RICK: More like, she made the grand entrance.
SAYLOR (VO): Ha, she can. I’m not surprised. Did she explain the situation?
RICK: Yes, she did.
SAYLOR (VO): Stellar. Now please, if I could join the operation, I would. But I’m stuck and I can’t delegate my stuff. Help Char reach the primary system and disable it. I’ll send the maps.
RICK: Gotcha.
SAYLOR (VO): Apparently it’s urgent, and I wasn’t ready for it. Thankfully Charlie is, so don’t waste time.
RICK: I heard you.
SAYLOR (VO): Don’t let me down.
RICK: (ends the call, talks to CHARLOTTE) Fine. Set the coordinates.

3000s. The sleepwalker warehouse at night.

CHARLOTTE and RICK are slowly walking through rows upon rows of the inactive sleepwalker androids, the very androids SAYLOR and STELLE met earlier at the same place.

CHARLOTTE and RICK are talking very quietly, looking everywhere, shining their flashlights from one corner to the other.

CHARLOTTE: These corridors are long, dark and incredibly boring… Rick! Ask me something.
RICK: Sorry, what?
CHARLOTTE: Just anything. Any question. I can’t believe that I’m actually dying of boredom right now.
RICK: Come on, Char, you’re dramatizing. What’s gonna happen if you take two more minutes of silence?
CHARLOTTE: My mind would derail. Anything can happen. There was a moment when I casually calculated the volume of three merged rhombicosidodecahedrons. All in my mind.
RICK: Rhombi-what?
CHARLOTTE: I can only pronounce it once. See? It’s started!
RICK: You made it up.
CHARLOTTE: Nah, it’s a geometric figure with 62 faces, 12 of which are pentagonal— Rick, I must stop rambling!
RICK: Stop rambling? Have you heard yourself?
CHARLOTTE: Rick, please!
RICK: Fine, fine, fine. Uh… You haven’t told me where exactly you’re from. You know, your real origins. We traveled a lot, but you’re hiding this exact piece. Why?
CHARLOTTE: I’m… from the 59th century.
RICK: 59th century… Well, with your technology, it figures.
CHARLOTTE: It’s not that simple, Rick.
RICK: Can we go there someday?
CHARLOTTE: No. Absolutely not. I forbid that. Only over my dead body. This is one place I’m never going to if I really value my life.
RICK: What, is it that bad?
CHARLOTTE: See for yourself. (pulls the old PTT out of her pocket) This PTT.
RICK: What about it?
CHARLOTTE: I don’t even own it.
RICK: You’re mad! You’re just like Saylor!!
CHARLOTTE: I know.
RICK: You stole it! You stole it and you’re never returning it! Finders-keepers, eh, Charlie?
CHARLOTTE: Yes. We had time travel training in the high school. I used this old model for 7 years.
RICK: 7 years… Must be the warranty has expired.
CHARLOTTE: Warranty void immediately after a single unauthorized and unguided teleportation. (puts the old PTT back in the pocket) Well, I was 15. A perfect moment for us little sweet naive fools to start learning to fly in time and space. We had our instructors. My model glitched out, a rare occasion, and I was transported to the Mesozoic Era.
RICK: Mesozoic… At 15…
CHARLOTTE: Only later I realized that it was a part of the course. I had to get back to get my grades. But once I figured out what I have on my wrist, and realized its potential, I decided: no, I’m going to the 19th century. I had nothing that held me in the 59th century anyway, nothing of value. No friends, and my parents… Heh, you wish you never had them…
RICK: So that’s why you chose the Victorian aesthetics!
CHARLOTTE: I’ve read the books. One thing I’m infinitely grateful to my mom and dad is a big personal library. There were a few books about Victorian flair, I’ve read them with delight and wonder, savoring every word, like a hungry child who has spotted a plump turkey. And then, I discovered a city library…
RICK: A bookworm’s paradise…
CHARLOTTE: So much literature with Victorian aesthetics. I remember I even had Victorian club, but no one was interested. I was the odd one out.
RICK: And the 19th century changed everything.
CHARLOTTE: People noticed me! The nobles! They thought I was a lost orphan.
RICK: Can’t blame them.
CHARLOTTE: Instantly, balls, friends, cute dresses, beautiful manners, high society!
RICK: I remember.
CHARLOTTE: Of course you remember! You’ve met them! I even introduced you to Ada Lovelace. Saylor would never do that.
RICK: Of course she never would. She tries to avoid meddling with history.
CHARLOTTE: And Ada was my friend.
RICK: I hope you didn’t explain your parasol to her.
CHARLOTTE: Well, she slightly contributed to it, but don’t worry, she never understood how it works.
RICK: Thank goodness.
CHARLOTTE: (linking arms) Remember our waltz, Rick?
RICK: Oh boy…
CHARLOTTE: Don’t deny it, you were doing good.
RICK: Nah.
CHARLOTTE: Well, Lisa and Saylor told me the same. When it was their turn to dance with you.
RICK: By the way, why were the three random girls huddled together off to the side that day, gossiping while the dance was going on?
CHARLOTTE: Come on, we were scandalous! Also, not random. Rose, Violet, and Lavender. Please respect my pals.
RICK: Sorry, you have thousands of friends and the list keeps expanding. It’s hard to keep track.
CHARLOTTE: That’s why, Ricky, we have to travel more often. You have to socialize just like I do.
RICK: About scandalous. Not “we were.”
CHARLOTTE: Right. _You_ were.
RICK: Impossible.
CHARLOTTE: One of them was deeply moved, so they were talking to each other to cool her blush.
RICK: Er, moving on. Moving on right now.
CHARLOTTE: (chuckles) Yeah, let’s not talk about how it all delightfully ended, ha-ha.
RICK: Never remember this ever again.
CHARLOTTE: I’ll do better than that. I’ll go and tell her that you’re actually interested.
RICK: _NO!_
CHARLOTTE: (operating PTT) I’m gonna pre-set the configurations…
RICK: Hey. Your newer PTT model. Surely you got it _after_ you ran away from that dreadful 59th century.
CHARLOTTE: Newer PTT is from the 61st century. Consumer-grade technology by then, but extremely unaffordable.
RICK: Stole it again.
CHARLOTTE: No, I bought it. With the proper license.
RICK: Ah…
CHARLOTTE: It’s unaffordable in terms of potential risks involved. But still. Always great to see that times change and all policies of one century are getting wiped away by the next. I’m not in the watchlist in there anymore, since technically, from the official documents alone, I’m considered deceased.
RICK: Dark.
CHARLOTTE: It’s bureaucracy.
RICK: Wait. But what documents are you using to visit the 61st century legally?
CHARLOTTE: They’re still figuring out how to police time travelers. What guardrails to apply. They have no idea.
RICK: Try to catch a fly with bare hands.
CHARLOTTE: Exactly.

## P.S.

1838, London palace.

Flash, bang in the hallway. RICK and CHARLOTTE appeared at some door in the Victorian palace, linking arms. Dressed for the occasion.

CHARLOTTE: Phew! Well? Saylor is safe. Once the sleepwalker attack happens in a few hours…
RICK: …self-destruct will be triggered automatically.
CHARLOTTE: Exactly!

CHARLOTTE and RICK high-five.

RICK: So. This is your idea of having a break, then. Your favorite era.
CHARLOTTE: It’s therapeutic, come on.
RICK: No, “therapeutic” refers to spa treatments. Your idea of “therapeutic” is being an emotional vampire to everyone around you. Especially when I’m around. You literally feed off people’s reactions to my nonsense.
CHARLOTTE: Which is why I like you.
RICK: Don't let your guard down.
CHARLOTTE: Right, this is it. That’s the door.
RICK: (recognizing the door) No. No, Char, let’s step away. Do you really want to disturb her?
CHARLOTTE: That’s not disturbance when it’s _you_ who is demanding attention.
RICK: And I’m not!
CHARLOTTE: I’ll say the opposite.
RICK: Please don’t.
CHARLOTTE: Remember two things we talked about?
RICK: (sighs) Remind me.
CHARLOTTE: You need to socialize, and you’re doing it under my guidance.
RICK: And second?
CHARLOTTE: I don’t take “no” for an answer.

CHARLOTTE knocks on the door.

LAVENDER (OS): Come in!
CHARLOTTE: (to RICK) Well? This is it. She’s waiting. Step up. I need to see my friend brave.
RICK: (collects himself) To hell with this.

RICK enters first, CHARLOTTE follows him.

RICK: Good evening.
LAVENDER: (gasps) You. And Charlotte? I couldn’t possibly expect your visit. Something of importance?
CHARLOTTE: Good evening. I must inform with my utmost excitement that my closest friend Rick expresses the exceptional interest in… well, let him proceed in his own words.
RICK: (fixes his collar) Uh… Uh, conversation! (CHARLOTTE is slowly facepalming) Yes, I’m interested in having a conversation. Classic, friend-to-friend, nothing more and nothing less, ha-ha.
LAVENDER: I don’t think that’s the actual intention of your visit, Rick. I noticed the deflection in your voice. May I expect more transparency from a person I…? Ahem.
CHARLOTTE: (whispering with growing excitement) She snapped. She snapped! _She snapped!!_ (linking arms, turns deadpan, slowly walks closer to LAVENDER) I noticed your acceptance and, as Rick’s closest companion, I’m entrusting him to your safe care. (whispering to RICK) If something goes wrong, I’ll protect you. Come on! (looks at LAVENDER and smiles)

RICK wants to look down at the floor, but CHARLOTTE immediately lifts his head, demanding that he look directly at LAVENDER.

RICK: The… conversation is personal and the subject of the conversation is you. (covers his mouth with his hand)
LAVENDER: (chuckles) I see your complexion is changing.
RICK: (mouth shut) It’s not me, it’s Charlotte.
CHARLOTTE: Mm-hm, I haven’t planted this idea, it was a genuine response. (unlinking the arms and stepping back) I’m leaving you alone.
RICK: (mouth shut, looks back at CHARLOTTE) What are you doing?!
CHARLOTTE: It doesn’t matter what I’m doing. Look at what _she_ will do. Bye!!

CHARLOTTE sprints to the door and goes out. RICK looks nervously at LAVENDER.

LAVENDER: (reaches out to move RICK’s hand away from his face) If I may…
RICK: Fine. We have some misunderstanding.

Hallway. As soon as CHARLOTTE exits and closes the door, she opens the door just a little and looks through the narrow opening. Back to the room.

LAVENDER: Misunderstanding? Am I supposed to believe this? Lay all the cards out.
RICK: Lady Lavender, I wasn’t prepared for this. Charlotte had a wild idea. I was dragged along.
LAVENDER: And what’s your suggestion? Because if you’re leaving now, you’re inarticulately expressing your disrespect.
RICK: I’m really trapped, aren’t I? (looks back at the door) Charlotte, you’re a fool!

Hallway. CHARLOTTE quietly giggles. Back to the room.

LAVENDER: You’re a strange person, Rick. Your behavior does not meet the standards of high society. And honestly…
RICK: Oh dear…
LAVENDER: That’s exactly why you caught my interest that day.
CHARLOTTE (behind the door): (gasps without voice) _Oh, my, blooming heck!!_
RICK: Well. Heh. I’m weird. Yes, I’m weird.
LAVENDER: You’re demonstrating your quirkiness even now. Is this your natural tendency?
RICK: Please don’t tell me you like it. Because I hate it.
LAVENDER: Pity. You should own it.
RICK: You really think so?
CHARLOTTE: (enters abruptly) For heaven’s sake, make a move!!
RICK & LAVENDER: Out!!!

Someone knocks on the door.

CHARLOTTE: Looks like we’re about to have a party.
LAVENDER: Enter.

ROSE and VIOLET are entering the room. As soon as they see LAVENDER and RICK, they both gasp.

VIOLET: I can’t believe it. Rose, are you seeing what I’m seeing?
ROSE: Your eyes don’t trick you, Violet.

ROSE and VIOLET are walking past CHARLOTTE closer to RICK and LAVENDER. They’re both standing on either side of RICK.

ROSE: My, my. That ball really left a lasting impression, didn’t it, Lavender?
VIOLET: Take a look at her confidence. Is this the same lady who needed a piece of ice to repaint her crimson?
ROSE: Rick, what have you done to make her change her behavior?
LAVENDER: He did nothing—
RICK: I can speak for myself, thank you very much!
CHARLOTTE: If I may say—
ROSE, VIOLET, RICK & LAVENDER: Out!!!
CHARLOTTE: Understood. Hey, I can uh, just stand here and listen, alright?
RICK: Ladies, I demand her immediate audience.
ROSE: Fine.
VIOLET: Now explain the situation.
RICK: Must I? Look, your gossip wasn’t unnoticed.
ROSE: Oh, look at him. First day in high society and this gentleman is spotting everything.
LAVENDER: I mean, Rose, Violet, let’s not fool ourselves. My behavior was noticeable from another end of the hall, and Rick was dancing with Charlotte mere yards away. You think he wouldn’t notice me?
RICK: Hey, hey, hey. Don’t make it personal. I simply participated in the event and you just happened to be an observer nearby. Nothing special.
CHARLOTTE: (pushes the back of RICK) No, sunshine, own it. You know exactly what happened.
RICK: Uh-huh. You tactically calculated your movements to appear near these ladies. You’ve set up the whole thing, you wanted to present myself closer, deliberately.
LAVENDER: And I’m not complaining. (pulls RICK by the hand so he’ll stand next to her) Ladies, I’m ready to make an official announcement. I hereby declare him my partner.
RICK: (covering his mouth with his hand, he loudly whispers to everyone else) Under my protest!
VIOLET: Rick, give up.
ROSE: We accept this.
RICK: No, no, ladies, be reasonable, you can’t be so easygoing, please!
ROSE: What could go wrong?
VIOLET: Well, except for wedding.
ROSE: Wedding, yes!
RICK: No!!!
CHARLOTTE: Stop this! Indeed, are you insane? Wedding? You really have empty heads, you’re literally incapable of thinking of anything else. You’re offending me.
RICK: Tell them, Char!
CHARLOTTE: He hasn’t met so many people yet, let him choose for himself! (pulling RICK’s arm) I’m taking my platonic partner back.
LAVENDER: (doesn’t let RICK go) He’s staying.
CHARLOTTE: Lavender!
RICK: Char. Just let’s roll with it. (winking)
CHARLOTTE: (catching the wink) What does that mean?
RICK: (whispering) Escape. Trust me.

CHARLOTTE winks back.

As soon as CHARLOTTE lets go of RICK’s hand, LAVENDER yanks his arm so sharply that their faces almost touch, but RICK manages to cover her mouth with his finger.

RICK: No. Listen to my verdict first before we reach mutual agreement.
LAVENDER: (pulls RICK’s finger away, but moves back a few inches) And your verdict is?
RICK: Give me time. Why have you made your decision so quickly, anyway?
LAVENDER: Who told you that the decision was quick? Charlotte told me the stories about you. To be honest, I’m surprised you two aren’t together yet. So I assumed you’re a difficult gentleman to approach.
CHARLOTTE: (stands between them, offended) What made you think I ever could be interested in this idiot?
LAVENDER: Dear Charlotte, I can’t find one corner of our society that wouldn’t know you can’t exist separately. We even have our saying: “You can find Rick by the scent of Charlotte’s perfume.”
RICK: My, rumors are spreading fast…
CHARLOTTE: Welcome to the high society.
RICK: (yawns) Yeah, yeah, society… Very high… High as the height at which you were hanging from your rope.
CHARLOTTE: Oh blooming heck, ladies, I absolutely forgot. He’s sleep-deprived. Allow me to take care. This is serious.
ROSE: Oh no…
VIOLET: What happened? It’s early.
RICK: How early?
VIOLET: Ten o’clock in the evening. Don’t you know?
CHARLOTTE: He’s uh, he’s been tired since he entered the room.
ROSE: So, what kind of trials did you put him through to squeeze every last drop out of him again?
CHARLOTTE: No time for a chat! He can barely stand on his own two feet, please help me take him to the room across from mine! Now!
LAVENDER: Rose, Violet, everyone, we’ll meet again in the morning. Leave me alone with my thoughts.
VIOLET: Fine. Charlotte, hold him on that side. I’ll take him on this side. Rose, keep an eye on him from behind.
ROSE: Understood.
RICK: Hey, don’t assist me. (almost faints for a brief second, but collects himself)
CHARLOTTE: Uh-huh, faceplanting would remind you of your fierce independence. Come on.

20 minutes later. Hallway. CHARLOTTE is about to leave her room. She stands in the doorway and cautiously looks around. After making sure the hallway is empty, she tiptoes over to the door on the other side, where RICK is. As soon as CHARLOTTE reached the door, it opened. RICK almost bumped into her.

CHARLOTTE: (high-pitched) Ooh—!
RICK: Sh-h.

Their conversation entirely turns into whisper.

RICK: All clear?
CHARLOTTE: Yes. Everyone must be dreaming at this moment. I hope.
RICK: What do you mean, you hope?
CHARLOTTE: Look. Never trust a woman in love. Lavender might have some insane midnight ideas.
RICK: How familiar.
CHARLOTTE: To be honest, I’m actually afraid of her.
RICK: Can we escape now? Put PTT to practical use—at least this once in your life!
CHARLOTTE: Don’t diss my PTT!!
RICK: Sh-h!
CHARLOTTE: We must stay till the morning. That’s the only fair play. If we’re expected to be present in their meeting, we must follow the etiquette. After all, Rick, let’s be completely honest.
RICK: About?
CHARLOTTE: This is not the last time we’re here. Far from it. Both of us are already an integral part of this society. It’s not like we can run away to another century and permanently forget about this palace like a fever dream.
RICK: I see what you mean, Char, but I didn’t sign up for this!
CHARLOTTE: Sorry. You’ve heard the gossips.
RICK: And now one particular girl is hunting me down. You enabled her for months. Curse you, Lady Charlotte!
CHARLOTTE: Ooh, look at you, Gentleman Rick, calling me Lady for the first time. Intriguing.
RICK: (pushing CHARLOTTE towards her door) Get lost.
CHARLOTTE: (pulls RICK’s arm) Nah. Not this time. We fetch a few books from the library and a hot kettle. Because I’m extremely bored. How about we read a book together?

Behind them, a melodious, soft cough echoes. CHARLOTTE and RICK look back. They see VIOLET walking over to them.

VIOLET: (to RICK) It looks to me like you’ve been cured of your sleep deprivation.
RICK: Well. Sleep deprivation is turned into insomnia. It happens sometimes.
VIOLET: Ah. How timely. There’s a well-known lady among our mutual acquaintances who’s also suffering from this problem. Allow me to escort you, if you please.
RICK: Ahem. Lady Charlotte? If you excuse us.
CHARLOTTE: Oh. Oh, right. Expect my arrival in 10 minutes, I’ll be in my room.

RICK nods, CHARLOTTE leaves.

RICK: (to VIOLET) If you think I would fall for this transparent deception, you must be thinking of my utter incompetence.
VIOLET: Gentleman Rick. I owe you my personal apology for a jocular attitude. But at this moment, I mean serious talk, while my companions Lady Rose, Lady Lavender and Lady Charlotte are not present. You may expect my complete honesty.
RICK: My attention is all yours, Lady Violet. I’m listening.
VIOLET: Lady Lavender makes me worry. She pays you an excessive amount of attention, which has begun to affect our friendly and united community. I shall rely on your assistance, but if you accept the terms, may we agree to keep it private and unannounced?
RICK: Are you proposing a secret alliance?
VIOLET: Yes.
RICK: What am I supposed to do?
VIOLET: Find the ways to dampen her interest, in a natural, unforced way. But please. Avoid scandal. Lady Lavender values her reputation, and we don’t want her to lose face. So be delicate.
RICK: Understood. It’s for everyone’s good, it’s for the greater good of society. I accept the terms. Now, must I follow you to the Lady Lavender’s room?
VIOLET: No, it was a public deception maneuver. Return to your room safely. We shall continue our conversation in the morning.
RICK: I shall be looking forward to it.
VIOLET: Oh, and one more request.
RICK: Yes?
VIOLET: How would you feel about a proposal for lessons on etiquette and manners? You know we saw right through you at that dance and afterwards.
RICK: As for the fact that I’m an open book against my will? I’m aware, Lady Violet. But that’s _genuinely_ unexpected. Wait, private? Without Lady Charlotte, without anyone?
VIOLET: Obviously. Unless a narrowly focused expertise is required.
RICK: You really want to help me, then. To help me fit in seamlessly.
VIOLET: Your first appearance was… impressive. Very impressive. But you break the standards. This, in turn, affects the overall status. And I know you don’t mean it. You simply need a better guidance.
RICK: Thank you. Thank you, I accept your suggestion.
VIOLET: My pleasure. In this case, that’s all I have to offer.
RICK: Lady Violet! Does that mean we’re entering a space of mutual respect?
VIOLET: You should’ve seen that from day one, Gentleman Rick. Sometimes you have to set aside your views and look a person in the face. I had no malice in my eyes and my heart. Good night.

VIOLET leaves. RICK just stands, not moving, processing the situation. In a few seconds, CHARLOTTE returns with a stack of five books in her hands.

CHARLOTTE: Well, I gave up on tea for now, but— Uh, why are you still here? Aren’t you supposed to be stolen and eaten alive?
RICK: You’re projecting your worst nightmares on me. You’re reading too many books, perhaps. It spoils your imagination. See you in the morning.
CHARLOTTE: Hey! But what about Violet? Don’t tell me you already became besties behind my back!
RICK: That would be telling. Bye.

CHARLOTTE watches RICK going to his room. She shakes her head. As soon as she’s heading to her room, she’s being stopped by ROSE.

ROSE: Just a moment. Is he still there?
CHARLOTTE: Rose. Blooming heck, it’s one in the morning. For the love of Victoria, sleep.
ROSE: I’m aware of the conversation between him and Violet.
CHARLOTTE: Sigh… Girls. Even I have limits for chaotic performances. Sometimes it’s you who need pieces of ice to chill out.
ROSE: Bookworm. Bookworm as usual.
CHARLOTTE: Do not throw this title at my face. (looks at the books in her hands) Besides, French Revolution is a fascinating read. Especially from English perspective of this era. If you knew how some historical details were twisted and I haven’t even spotted it for years— Ah wait, your head can’t even comprehend what I’m saying. (quieter) Unlike Rick.
ROSE: Well, well. If you think you can fool me, you’re making a mistake. I can see a fresh layer of specific influence left on you. When Rick is absent, your behavior changes as if a magic spell had been lifted from you.
CHARLOTTE: Perceptive drama queen…

CHARLOTTE stands at the doorframe of her room, watching ROSE knock on the door of the RICK’s room. RICK opens the door.

RICK: Lady Rose? (noticing CHARLOTTE behind) Charlotte, if this is your another insane idea, I swear!
CHARLOTTE: Just listen to what she wants to deliver. I don’t even know with your team-ups. Figure things out between yourselves without me.
ROSE: Rick, I noticed you had a talk with Violet.
RICK: No formalities?
ROSE: Who would judge at a time like this?
CHARLOTTE: I won’t.
ROSE: Precisely.
CHARLOTTE: Yet.
ROSE: (pauses for half a second, then melodically coughs and continues) Rick, you’re an embarrassing dancer, do you know that?
CHARLOTTE: (quietly) Straight…
RICK: So?
ROSE: You certainly need to correct that. The next ball is in 9 days.
RICK: Who said I will take a part?
CHARLOTTE: Who said you won’t?
ROSE: Exactly. And, you need a guide.
RICK: Let me guess—
ROSE: I’m offering my help.
RICK: Char. I need you to invent a security system. A strong one. Right now.
CHARLOTTE: Why so dramatic?
RICK: If Lavender is going to be the next who offers her help, I swear. If you, Charlotte, entered through a window to pull me out into the outside world, I'm going to run away through a window in this century. We go full circle.
ROSE: Ah! Full circle, right! Allow me to demonstrate that my words are not empty.

ROSE raises her arms and, on her tiptoes, performs a smooth ballet turn in place.

ROSE: I’m a ballet dancer.
RICK: My god…
ROSE: Do you accept my training? The time is short.
RICK: Look. We have mere hours till the breakfast meeting. Surely we can discuss it at the tea party.
ROSE: Right. Until the morning, then.

CHARLOTTE goes to her room to leave the books, then she walks back toward the doorway.

RICK: Wait, I’m confused. I don’t understand. When we were in the Lavender’s room, you were ready to dissect me.
ROSE: Lavender is my closest friend. That’s how we communicate. Don’t mind our dynamic. It’s all in jest, really.
RICK: You joker…
ROSE: (chuckles) I suppose I am. Well, I don’t see any reason to hold you up. Good night.
RICK: Good night, indeed.

ROSE walks away. CHARLOTTE looks at RICK with smirk and crossed arms.

CHARLOTTE: Natural charmer, eh?
RICK: Shut up.
CHARLOTTE: Mind you, they’re quite the troublemakers.
RICK: They just took pity on me for my setbacks. Individually. That doesn’t mean anything serious.
CHARLOTTE: (laughs) We’ll see about that. Night-night.
RICK: Bye.

CHARLOTTE and RICK close the doors to their rooms.

15 minutes later. A woman's hand is knocking on RICK’s door. No response. Same hand knocks on the door again. RICK slowly opens the door. He’s very sleepy. As soon as he notices who knocked, he doesn’t react. He just pauses.

RICK: Oh no.
LAVENDER: I apologize for the inconvenience.

RICK is about to close the door, but LAVENDER holds the door.

LAVENDER: No, wait. Listen.
RICK: I’ve heard enough from you. More than enough. See you in the morning.
LAVENDER: Rick, I beg you, this is important.
RICK: Sigh… Lavender. It’s almost 2 in the morning. Nothing _socially_ important happens at this time. Except for three things: murder, fire, and a sleepwalker attack.
LAVENDER: _Whose_ attack?
RICK: The clockwork men. Who have been neutralized, for now. Now it’s _you_ who needs to be “neutralized.”
LAVENDER: Ah. I keep forgetting that you and Charlotte are calling them sleepwalkers. You really are brave handling them.
RICK: Lavender. What do you want?
LAVENDER: I want to apologize for my recent behavior. I should have given you some personal space and respect it. My attitude was a misstep. I went too far.
RICK: You can’t backpedal from your own bold claim that I’m officially your partner. And I must highlight this explicitly: I didn’t express my consent.
LAVENDER: Listen. I had to stop suppressing my feelings. For my own sanity. Please respect my openness, expressive as it is. Rick, for the last week, I was burning inside. I couldn’t help myself.
RICK: You’ve made quite a catastrophe out of this situation, you know that?
LAVENDER: I accept that I committed it, and I own it. Can you stop closing your eyes on this?
RICK: Fine. Fine. You’ve backed me into a corner, can’t you see? I don’t even know how to react. I said earlier, you should give me time. Now it looks like you want to expedite the consideration.
LAVENDER: Of course I want to expedite! I’ve already decided everything I possibly can for myself. Rick, how much longer do I have to wait?
RICK: But what about _me_?!
LAVENDER: Rick. You won’t break. You’re stronger than you appear to be. Me? I hardly control myself when I know you’re somewhere in this palace.
RICK: Please respect my time and my agency. I beg you, Lavender. I have no intention to continue until the clock strikes nine. I’ll see you later.
LAVENDER: Rick!

RICK closes the door.

LAVENDER: (knocks on the door) Rick, please, get back! (knocks again) Do you hear me?

LAVENDER slowly lowers her hand. Then she lowers her head. She slowly walks away, visibly defeated.
