# Charlotte Adventures: Part 7 (Teaser)

A script draft for _Bombs vs. Sleepwalkers_. Written in **September 17, 2026** by **liledix⁴**.

----

1982, cafeteria.

RICK and CHARLOTTE are sitting at the table, dressed appropriately. There’s an apple pie on the table, partially eaten. Both of them are eating their slices.

CHARLOTTE: It’s been a while since I’ve been here. (stretches her arms upward) I’ll finally be able to stretch my legs. Very literally.
RICK: I keep forgetting you’re an actual performer.
CHARLOTTE: You would love to see my 1982 stand-up after my dance routine. I’ve already shown off my waltz skills. Now I’m going to show off my fiery dance moves on stage to synth-pop.
RICK: I’m already scared.
CHARLOTTE: There won’t be any chairs to hold onto, don’t worry. More to the point, I’ll meet you with Blondie. Hah, ironically enough.
RICK: Back to the fu— ahem, our task. So. That fella who constantly underestimates himself, you say.
CHARLOTTE: Yeah. Kind soul, honestly. Somehow you’re similar, like two kittens in a box, so both of you might find a common ground.
RICK: Ah. You assigned me a role of temporal therapist.
CHARLOTTE: You handled Celeste very decently. Now I can’t handle that guy. Why is there always a catch with the guys like you?
RICK: Hey! Don’t talk about me as if I represent a whole different species.
CHARLOTTE: Sometimes it feels like you do.
RICK: I can absolutely say the same about you.
CHARLOTTE: Oh, how wholesome. Mirror tactics.
RICK: Except you’re a representative of a dying breed. Fera Carola!
CHARLOTTE: (mock offended) Ouch, Ricky! Ouch!
RICK: They just haven’t gotten around to adding you to the Red List yet.
CHARLOTTE: (laughs, nudges) Shut up, Rickus Analyticus.
RICK: Okay, you know the current problem.
CHARLOTTE: Yes. You, darling, got my PTT. But you’ve gotten too used to me always dragging you along with me.
RICK: Oh wow, an acknowledgement. We’re reaching _that_ stage, aren’t we?
CHARLOTTE: (ignores the sarcasm) You need to get my mindset. Ask yourself, “What would I do if I were Charlotte?”
RICK: That’s a terrifying thought.
CHARLOTTE: I’m not trying to get inside your head.
RICK: Jokes on you, you’re already there.
CHARLOTTE: Navigation, Rick! You lack a sense of navigation!
RICK: You’re kind of right, I’ll give you that.
CHARLOTTE: Now. Try to be imaginative. Try to wonder what scene you’d like to appear. What situation you’d want to get involved in. Which cat you’d want to rescue from a tree.
RICK: It… takes some practice.
CHARLOTTE: Start with something small. Whatever you decide, I’m always literally attached to your arm to guide you.
RICK: Please don’t say the line…
CHARLOTTE: Never gonna give you up.
RICK: No!
CHARLOTTE laughs.
RICK: Besides, 5 years too early. Keep some historic accuracy.
CHARLOTTE: Oh so you know!
RICK: Anyways. Can I keep it untold?
CHARLOTTE: Oh! Brilliant! Surprise trip, then! I’m in.
RICK: But only after we finish with _this_ quest.
CHARLOTTE: Of course, Rick. Of course. Always focused, always alert. I like it.

They keep finishing their pie.

CHARLOTTE: But hey. If we go to Ancient Greece, we must meet Sususs Amongus.
RICK: I keep telling you, this person doesn’t exist!
CHARLOTTE: You’ll be surprised, sweetie.
RICK: And who else exists?
CHARLOTTE: Catshepsut in Ancient Egypt.
RICK: Please stop.
CHARLOTTE: No, I’m serious, hidden sides of history are _that_ wild! History books record only the most notable events from a limited perspective. Reality, however, has a way of surprising us.
RICK: I bet you’ve found something ridiculous in the French Revolution.
CHARLOTTE: You can only imagine.
