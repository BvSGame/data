# The Rogue Sleepwalker

A script draft for _Secretly Inc._, the _Bombs vs. Sleepwalkers_ spin-off. Written in **August 13, 2026** by **liledix⁴**.

----

2024, the top secret agency. The office of the agent MARIE.

MARIE is 21 years old, she wears corporate-style outfit: sleeveless buttoned-up shirt, black tie, pencil skirt, tactical boots. Her hair is shoulder-length blonde with flippy ends.

For this moment, she sits at the table and writing some papers.

Someone knocks on the door.

MARIE: Come in.

The CLIENT who enters the office is a young, junior police officer.

CLIENT: Good afternoon. You’re Marie, right?
MARIE: Yes, it’s me. This is top security establishment, do you have a pass?
CLIENT: Yes, one moment… Uh… (shows the pass card) Here.
MARIE: Uh-huh… (takes the pass card and slides it through the verification device) Right. Legitimate. (returns the card) So. How can I help you, junior police officer?
CLIENT: Well, you were recommended. It’s been told that you’re one of the most productive agents in this establishment.
MARIE: That is correct.
CLIENT: You see, there is a case everyone tries to avoid studying.
MARIE: Regarding?
CLIENT: Mysterious robots. We have a critical lack of records in our system regarding them. A few of us tried to investigate, but it’s tough luck.
MARIE: (about to drink soda) We deal with robots every day. What’s the reason for your request?
CLIENT: This time, we have a murder.
MARIE: (frowns, pauses mid-sip) Continue.

10 minutes later. MARIE calls RICK.

RICK: Marie?! How did you call me? I’ve blocked six of your phone numbers.
MARIE: You really think I can’t bypass basic call blocking, sweetie?
RICK: I pay my money for the service.
MARIE: Your favorite service cooperates with our agency. Your app has so many backdoors that I can open a backdoor shop. Your data is about to become public domain if you ignore me. But you have a choice. If you buy me a fine wine and come down here, I may ban myself from invasions at least for a week. And I’m offering this seriously, because I’m strictly interested in the mission right now.
RICK: Uh-huh… Marie. In the context of my presence, your definition of “mission” is marital obligation, let’s not fool ourselves. I don’t know, sometimes I have a feeling that your parents named you specifically to unsettle me. Marie sounds way too close to “marry.”
MARIE: No. Read the room. I need you as an assistant with empirical experience.
RICK: Empirical experience? Your corporate side strikes again. Do you really expect me to fall for this?
MARIE: I’m serious. Right at this moment, I’ve got an authorization to the highly secure area from the higher-ups.
RICK: I know why. It’s your twisted way to isolate both of us from the rest of the civilization.
MARIE: No. It’s about something you may understand due to your education and time travel experience. The rogue sleepwalker.
RICK: The rogue sleepwalker?
MARIE: I’m sending you the briefing and a digital key to the secure area. Check the encrypted messenger.
RICK: Hmm… Is this key signed?
MARIE: Yes, I’ll send a certificate that proves it.
RICK: Okay, let me read your briefing and I’ll call you back.
MARIE: I’ll be waiting.

3 minutes later. RICK calls MARIE.

MARIE: Well?
RICK: Right. The key is legitimate. The briefing is concise but precise. But you can’t request access to the secure areas for petty trickery. This is a highly surveiled area, you may risk your career.
MARIE: That’s exactly the point, you fool! Just come here and you’ll see I mean you nothing personal.
RICK: Wait, I’ve got the message from Sasha.
MARIE: Finally she woke up…
RICK: Well. Alright. Looks like for once your request is legitimate.
MARIE: You’re insulting my competence.
RICK: I’ll be in 10 minutes.
MARIE: I shall be waiting.

12 minutes later, RICK (20 years old) appears in the agency facility. MARIE waits leaning back against the door to her office, crossed her arms.

MARIE: You’re 2 minutes late.
RICK: Wine took a bit to manage.
MARIE: (hissing) Not here! Into the office. Leave it there and we’ll go to the secure area immediately.
RICK: Fine, fine.

MARIE and RICK enter the office. RICK puts a bottle of wine on the table.

RICK: There. As you ordered. You really had to fake my identity for me to be able to buy you a drink? You’re impossible.
MARIE: This is how we deal with things before your actual birthday hits.
RICK: Yeah, Marie… You’re outdoing yourself. I don’t know how you stay in this job with all this facade of a professional agent.
MARIE: Easily. I have a few convincing cover stories up my sleeve.
RICK: (points at Marie’s bare arms, the lack of sleeves) If you had one.
MARIE: Don’t be clever.
RICK: Why not?
MARIE: (slowly walking closer) You’re piquing my interest.
RICK: (turning to the door) Right, let’s move on to the area of yours.
MARIE: (right behind the back) And then we’ll get back to business in this office.
RICK: (opens the door, slightly annoyed) How else would I manage to get out of this agency? I know you too well.

2 minutes later. MARIE and RICK are walking down the corridor.

MARIE: Rogue sleepwalkers. What do you know about them?
RICK: Androids that went out of control and gained independence.
MARIE: And they’re murderous.
RICK: Not all of them. Far from it. A rare rogue would be down to outright killing.
MARIE: I need to show you one of the most dangerous kinds we managed to capture, neutralize and isolate.
RICK: Now you’re talking.
MARIE: I know your interests.

They enter the isolated area where the disabled rogue sleepwalker is stored.

RICK: Oh boy… This model is different.
MARIE: Have you met it before?
RICK: Never. It’s new to me. I don’t know if Saylor recognizes it.
MARIE: Genuinely it’s been a miracle to transport this one in here.
RICK: How did you manage to neutralize it?
MARIE: A few successful EMP blasts. But we had to misfire a lot. This thing is physically erratic. I wouldn’t dare to come closer even in this state.
RICK: (walks closer to the sleepwalker) I know what I’m doing.
MARIE: (grabs RICK’s arm) No, seriously. Be careful.
RICK: Marie. I’m a student of the technical university. More than that, I’m Saylor’s classmate and surrogate brother. (pulls MARIE’s hand away) I can manage.
