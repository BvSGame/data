# Metallic Madman

A script draft for _Bombs vs. Sleepwalkers_. Written in **August 5–6, 2026** by **liledix⁴**.

----

## Regular version

SAYLOR: Ah! Dumbbell! A pretentious rogue sleepwalker who got speech synthesis. A less boring one. I’m still sticking to my nickname.
STELLE: Mom, it’s called differently. It’s CHA-05-SF6EF.
SAYLOR: Hush.
DUMBBELL: Saylor! Finally you stepped into a trap.
SAYLOR: (looking at her fingernails) Oh. Try to impress me this time.
STELLE: (half-voiced) Why are you saying this?
SAYLOR: (half-voiced) Every time I’m trapped, I’m getting out of it. This routine is boring me already.
STELLE: (half-voiced) Show-off.
DUMBBELL: Scarlett is going to fail.
SAYLOR: Hm? Oh, tell me about it. There’s Charlotte who jumps with her in time and space, and Marie who is assisting her with information you prefer to hide. Our girl now has three times more power, and the best bit? You know it. You expect it. Scarlett is transparent as glass, but it only helps you to do the countdown. No need to scream, it's too late.
DUMBBELL: And your function?
SAYLOR: Setting a good example. Showing my beloved daughter how girls beat shiny metal bottoms. And we have a decent material.
DUMBBELL: We’ll see who gets beaten. You’re fragile. I can be rebuilt. You're in a losing battle.
SAYLOR: Oh, so we’re bragging about lack of bleeding now, isn't it? Are you really going to stoop that low?
DUMBBELL: Yes, lack of blood gives me quite an advantage.
SAYLOR: (snorts) Ah, advantage. Clean floors when someone slaps you a little too hard, I imagine.
STELLE: (hissing) Mom!
SAYLOR: Sounds like my advantage. No cleaning.
DUMBBELL: Incorrect. The advantage is… I can act cold-blooded.
SAYLOR: Huh. “Act” is a key word.

The big screen shows RICK tied to a chair. He doesn't hear what SAYLOR, STELLE and DUMBBELL say.

SAYLOR: Rick!
STELLE: Where is he? We were looking for him everywhere.
DUMBBELL: He’s been kept safe. Until you arrived.
SAYLOR: Whatever madness and intertwined wires you have in your mock-up of a head, leave him alone! Set him free, deal with _me_!
DUMBBELL: I _am_ dealing with you. Look at him.
STELLE: Oh, poor Rick…

RICK gets zapped, but he remains conscious. DUMBBELL looks at SAYLOR with amusement.

SAYLOR: (lower voice) I mean it. Leave him alone.

DUMBBELL grins and gets ready for the next zap.

SAYLOR: Are you deaf?! Leave him alone!!!

And RICK is zapped again.

SAYLOR: What the hell are you doing to him, you metal fool? Do you realize he’s _human_?!
RICK: Oh… Oh you really are an idiot. You can't harm Saylor with this!
SAYLOR: Jokes are over… (strides to the corridor) Rick! Rick, we’ll find you! We'll find you right now!! I’m coming!
DUMBBELL: Nuh-uh!

SAYLOR collides with an invisible force field.

SAYLOR: What do you want, you cretin?
DUMBBELL: Keep watching! I haven't finished yet.

RICK is zapped harder. RICK is visibly exhausted.

SAYLOR: No!!!
RICK: Saylor. I know you're hearing me somewhere. Help me. Please help me, Saylor, I don’t know how long I can handle this.
SAYLOR: (voice breaks) Brother.
STELLE: (half-voiced) Surrogate brother, but still hits…
RICK: I beg you. Find a way.
SAYLOR: I-I need some clues. Where are you?
RICK: Saylor. Please.

Longer zap. RICK loses consciousness. A few seconds of heavy silence as STELLE and SAYLOR are standing speechless.

STELLE: (nervous) He can't be… He’s… He’s just unconscious, right?… Mom. Mom, say something.

DUMBBELL laughs.

SAYLOR: (quiet, dead serious) You leave me no choice.
DUMBBELL: Finally. The language I can speak.
SAYLOR: (raising the voice) Speak it while you can. If you think you can get away with this, you’re making a grave mistake.
DUMBBELL: (chuckles) Oh, Saylor, please.
SAYLOR: (playfully) Saylor? Who is Saylor? I don’t see any Saylor here. (to STELLE) Do you know who Saylor is? (to DUMBBELL) May I congratulate you?

SAYLOR strides to DUMBBELL.

SAYLOR: (quiet, dead serious) You’ve unleashed your worst nightmare.

DUMBBELL abruptly stops making any sound and watches attentively. SAYLOR walks back to STELLE. She spreads her arms.

SAYLOR: Denim overall dress. Stelle, sweetie, do you know why it's better than the average dress?
STELLE: (eyerolling) It has pockets.
SAYLOR: Exactly. Pockets. (spinning in place, stealthily puts her hand in her pocket) Nice, deep, goody-good pockets. They’re deep enough to store…

SAYLOR abruptly pulls some device out of her pocket and throws it at DUMBBELL.

SAYLOR: …this!

DUMBBELL gets continuously electrocuted.

SAYLOR: A pocket-sized electromagnetic pulse! A portable electric anti-electronic bomb! Ha-ha-ha-ha-ha!!

SAYLOR slowly walks towards DUMBBELL that is still electrocuted.

SAYLOR: Oh I love 2345 for this!

And DUMBBELL falls. EMP finished its work. SAYLOR walks close, and STELLE follows.

SAYLOR: It doesn’t defeat a robotic entity, but it immobilizes. Now the mic is yours, speak your language. Where is Rick?
DUMBBELL: (weakened) Tough! Keep guessing. I won't tell you in a million years.
SAYLOR: Oh, I believe it won't take that long. Stelle? We’re doing some speedrunning.

STELLE pulls out a tablet and a data extractor cable from SAYLOR’s handbag and passes them to SAYLOR.

SAYLOR: Thank you. Now, let's take a look at your brain without your consent.
DUMBBELL: If you think I can’t employ a passive electrocution—
SAYLOR: (connects the data extraction cable to DUMBBELL and her tablet) Your defensive systems are temporarily non-operational, thank you very much. You’ve already used one shock tactic on me. It won’t happen again.
STELLE: So you don't think he's dead.
SAYLOR: I think nothing. I think the sooner we’ll find at least a small clue about where Rick is, the higher are our chances to save him.
STELLE: Look! Your software is generating a local map of this area.
SAYLOR: Stellar. So you do have something, hm? Little Scrappy? Expected to beat me, huh?
DUMBBELL: Enjoy your small victory while it lasts.
SAYLOR: Boo boo boo. I’ve got everything now. And even some extra. Oh, you naughty boy. Graphic bits will be deleted. Now! Stelle? Do you know about another advantage of a pocket EMP?

STELLE walks freely past the place where SAYLOR collided with the force field earlier.

SAYLOR: Indeed! Good girl. (to DUMBBELL) Bad boy. (to STELLE, arm inviting to the corridor) To the rescue.

Some time later (I’ll write the rescue operation in another edit).

SAYLOR and STELLE are holding RICK as they walk. They return to the “trap.”

As DUMBBELL notices them, he stands up slowly and abruptly, he’s severely weakened and partially disassembled, wires are hanging loose.

DUMBBELL: So. You think you’ve finished. I thought Rick could do better.

STELLE looks at SAYLOR. SAYLOR nods. STELLE grabs the compact EMP pistol from SAYLOR’s denim dress pocket.

The girl in green sundress points the compact EMP pistol at DUMBBELL.

STELLE: Dude. Behave.

STELLE shoots, DUMBBELL falls again.

Trio keeps moving.

## Alternative version

DUMBBELL: Scarlett is going to fail.
SAYLOR: Hm? Oh, tell me about it. (rummages through her pockets) Allow me to demonstrate…
STELLE: (to DUMBBELL, half-voiced) You better don’t make my kangaroo mom do the pocket show. I suspect her pockets are bigger on the inside.

SAYLOR takes a tiny gadget out of her pocket, holds the button until it beeps, drops it onto the floor, then operates her smartwatch (seemingly to make a call).

SAYLOR: There.

A full-sized hologram of two women appears: CHARLOTTE and SCARLETT. They’re wearing evening dresses. Seems like it’s yet another “fun-time” initiative of CHARLOTTE’s.

CHARLOTTE-HG: (gasps) Oh! Oh blooming heck! Saylor!
SCARLETT-HG: You do know that you must _warn_ us before initiating the holo-conference, right?
SAYLOR: Sorry, sorry. I have a point to make.
CHARLOTTE-HG: You always have. Time your calls correctly! If you called a few hours ago, when we were in the locker room, that would be embarrassing. (uses her PTT as the smartwatch) Lisa! Update my firewall and holo-conf app privacy! Make it reject the unprompted calls!
DUMBBELL: Indeed, it proves enough. I don’t know what it proves, but it’s quite amusing.
SAYLOR: Stelle? Shut him up. Do my trick for me. There’s a good gal.
DUMBBELL: No tricks!
STELLE: I didn’t ask you, android.

STELLE reaches for SAYLOR’s pocket, pulls out the pocket EMP and throws it at DUMBBELL to electrocute it.

STELLE: Count pi digits before your shutdown.

Then STELLE grabs the tablet and data extraction cable from SAYLOR’s handbag and runs to DUMBBELL to extract data from it after EMP finishes its work.

SAYLOR: May I ask you kindly if there’s a reason behind this ensemble of yours?
CHARLOTTE-HG: We’re having fun.
SAYLOR: With the most serious agent.
SCARLETT-HG: Off-duty.
SAYLOR: It’s late afternoon.
CHARLOTTE-HG: Oh, Saylor, don’t make me think that you’re unfamiliar with the concept of time zones.
SAYLOR: Don’t complicate the matter. Where and when are you?
SCARLETT-HG: Tokyo. Japan. Night in April 2048.
CHARLOTTE-HG: It’s blooming sakura.

Holograms of CHARLOTTE and SCARLETT are laughing (because CHARLOTTE avoided saying “blooming heck”).

SAYLOR: Charley, you’re such a definition of making friends along the way.
CHARLOTTE-HG: Hey! Mind joining us?
STELLE: (from the distance) I would be glad.
SAYLOR: Girls! Do you understand that we have a mission?
MARIE-HG: (appears, side-hugs SCARLETT) It’s been completed. Rick is safe.
SAYLOR: Ah, I see. You’re talking from my own future. The near future where Rick is rescued.
SCARLETT-HG: Why do you have to overcomplicate things?
SAYLOR: Because we’re time travelers! Things are always getting complicated for us!
CHARLOTTE-HG: Actually, we’re talking from your present. Trust the experienced time traveler, I know the perspective.
SAYLOR: Okay, but that Dumbbell kidnapped Rick. He needs to be rescued right now.
CHARLOTTE-HG: No, no, you’ve got it all wrong. He wasn’t kidnapped.
STELLE: (walks back to SAYLOR) Explain.
SCARLETT-HG: (nudges MARIE) Ahem?
MARIE-HG: I’ve done some research—
SCARLETT-HG: Stop fooling people. And stop being professional when I’m around.
MARIE-HG: Sorry. I… used an old rogue sleepwalker from the archives of our agency and created a fake recording of his torture.
STELLE: Torture?
MARIE-HG: Well, he’s still tortured but… rather psychologically. In my office. I locked him up and forced him to think carefully about interior design of our future apartment.
STELLE: “Our” future apartment?
SAYLOR: (half-voiced to STELLE) Rick is her ex when we were school classmates and now she stalks him to demand marriage. Unhinged behavior, long story, remind me to tell you the full version later.
SCARLETT-HG: (lets out a little laugh) This girl is helpless. My attention is not enough, is it? I’ll deal with Rick for you, don’t you worry.
SAYLOR: But why did you have to go to such lengths to cover up your… obsession? A robot? Fake data?
MARIE-HG: Because I’m a paranoid and I had to win this time at least.
SCARLETT-HG: Tsk tsk. Brutal. But hey. Saylor. Relax. Everything is under my control. I’m healing Marie. She clearly overworked and urgently needed some rest.
MARIE-HG: My official vacation starts in 73 days—
SCARLETT-HG: I decide when your vacation starts. You’re incapable of taking a rest on your own.
MARIE-HG: (to SAYLOR) And you say she isn’t bossy off-duty. Look at her demands. She wants to control my life. So which one of us is obsessed now?
CHARLOTTE-HG: (lets go of SCARLETT; holograms of SCARLETT and MARIE disappeared) Whatever, girls, you figure out your relationships without us.
SAYLOR: So you fooled me…
CHARLOTTE-HG: It was a part of Scarlett’s psy-op training. Discuss this with her later.
SAYLOR: I surely will. If she thinks she’s tough on-duty, we’ll see about that.
CHARLOTTE-HG: I don’t like your serious tone.
STELLE: Neither do I.
CHARLOTTE-HG: (operates PTT) I’m about to land…

Hologram of CHARLOTTE presses the tip of her parasol against PTT’s metal surface. Zap! Hologram disappeared. Flash and bang in the room!

CHARLOTTE: (physical, echoing) …now.

CHARLOTTE notices the lying, partially disassembled robot.

CHARLOTTE: Oh.

...and kicks it hard with her heeled shoe. With a loud hollow metal bang.

Then, she quickly strides to SAYLOR and STELLE.

CHARLOTTE: I don’t care what you say right now. I’m not listening. I’m taking you to change your dresses. Either five of us will conquer Tokyo in terms of fashion, or I’ll deal with Rick myself.
SAYLOR: Bad joke.
CHARLOTTE: Then don’t make me joke and listen to me! Mad Auntie Cherry steals you for cherry blossoms! Something you’ll never regret seeing. (passes parasol to STELLE) Sunshine, can you hold this for me?
STELLE: (takes it) Oh yes, please.
CHARLOTTE: (operates PTT and side-hugs both SAYLOR and STELLE tightly) Now, Stelle, press the soft deploy button on the parasol and press its tip against the metal surface, it receives the electrical power. Don’t confuse with the hard deployment, it’s for electrocution. Don’t turn us into fried chickens, I’m not sure if Japan tolerates this kind of KFC.
SAYLOR: I hate the mental image, thanks.
STELLE: (does what CHARLOTTE said) Zap!

And they disappeared with the flash and bang.
