# Two Shades of Black

A script draft for _Bombs vs. Sleepwalkers_. Written in **August 19–20, 2026** by **liledix⁴**. Edited in **August 21, 2026**.

----

## Cast

- **SKYLER (MALE)**—freelance investigator from 2038. Mid-20s. Black short hair. On-duty: wears black pants, loose black blazer, white shirt (buttoned-up except for top button), no tie. At home: wears black T-shirt and black shorts with drawstrings.
- **SKYLER (FEMALE)**—freelance investigator from 2038. Mid-20s (one year older than SKYLER (MALE)). Black long hair, loose, no styling. Wears the exact same outfit as SKYLER (MALE), with the exact same features like undone button on a shirt.
- **SCARLETT**—formally, field operative of the top secret agency in 2038, a saboteur. Unofficially, member of the Resistance. 24. Tall, athletic. Ice-cold serious. Blonde (loose on-duty, tied back to ponytail off-duty). Off-duty, she wears white tank top and black leggings.
- **SAYLOR**—tech university student from 2024. Time traveler. 20. Sarcastic technology maniac, bubblegum-flavor ambassador. Medium height. Shoulder-length peachy pink beachy bob, with purple and blue ends. Wears denim overall dress with sticky patches, multicolored sleeveless top underneath, light sneakers, star-shaped earrings, clock-shaped necklace, and smartwatch. Carries a handbag with a powerful tablet. Generally treated as the leading figure of the Resistance.
- **LISA**—human-realistic android (built in 2345) who is also a digital assistant (when she downloads herself into devices). Appears as an 18-year-old girl. Tall. Long black hair with slight red tint, tied back to a ponytail with a big green bow. Wears velvet blazer, white buttoned-up shirt, green neck bow, pleated skirt, stockings, medium-height stilettos.
- **RICK**—tech university student from 2024. Time traveler. 20. Slightly taller than medium height. Brown hair, chin-length, slightly messy. Wears denim jacket, T-shirt, dark jeans, and sneakers.
- **ALICE**—self-proclaimed expert in explosives from the 3000s. 19. Childish, but very energetic. Short height. Long loose brown hair, messy. Wears red T-shirt, shorts, and light sneakers.
- **MARIE**—analyst operative of the top secret agency in 2024. 21. Medium height. Flippy blonde shoulder-length hair. Wears sleeveless buttoned-up shirt, tie, pencil skirt, and tactical boots.
- **STELLE**—artificially grown human from the 14th Millennium. Saylor’s biological daughter due to DNA properties. Biologically 16, but lives in an outside world for approximately a week. A prodigy. Medium height. Long wavy brown hair. Wears green sundress, triangular earrings, green low-heeled shoes, and smartwatch.
- **CHARLOTTE**—independent time traveler with Victorian aesthetics. 22. Medium blonde hair with curly ends. Slightly taller than medium height. Wears practical outfit with Victorian patterns—brown-and-red shirt (three top buttons undone), dark blue trousers, medium-heeled shoes, Portable Time Transporter (PTT) strapped to her left forearm, and a multi-functional parasol.
- **BLAKE**—the actual leader of the Resistance. Middle-aged male. Wearing strict official outfit.

2038, Resistance HQ. 1 AM. Outdoors.

We see two persons, mid-20s male and female. Both of them are wearing semi-formal black outfit, with blazers and white buttoned-up shirts (the top button is undone), without ties. Their hair is also black, the only difference is that male has short hair and female has long (loose, no ponytail, no special style).

They’re heading toward the front doors of HQ. They talk quietly, but looks like they have a heated argument.

???? (MALE): Look. This is a foolish idea. Couldn’t we wait until afternoon?
???? (FEMALE): We have no opportunity for waiting. We must act as soon as humanly possible.
???? (MALE): I understand your operational efficiency, but “humanly possible” also means living like an actual human. It’s time to sleep like an actual human!
???? (FEMALE): I don’t disagree with you, but can we make an exception just this once?
???? (MALE): We made exceptions like what, 12 times already?
???? (FEMALE): It’s perfectly normal, you can’t operate perfectly by the book.
???? (MALE): In a row? Over the course of 3 days?
???? (FEMALE): Okay, let’s draw the line. Thirteenth exception is the last. For now. Then we follow the rules exactly as we established.
???? (MALE): I’ll take you at your word.
???? (FEMALE): So will I. Knock.
???? (MALE): But it’s your turn.
???? (FEMALE): Whatever. We’re even for now.
???? (BOTH): Let’s do in unison.
???? (FEMALE): And that was my idea first.
???? (MALE): You trickster.
???? (FEMALE): (chuckles proudly)

They both knock on the door.

SAYLOR (OS): Come on, stop doing that! We have an intercom here!
???? (MALE): Sorry, I'll keep that in mind! You’re Saylor, the leader of the Resistance, am I right?
SAYLOR (OS): Misinformation! I’m not the leader. But I’m Saylor.
LISA (OS): Just go and open that door!
SAYLOR (OS): And if they mean harm? Think about operational efficiency.

The door opens.

SCARLETT: Good night. I hope you have a decent reason to disturb us at the off-duty time. How can we help you?
SAYLOR: (from the distance) Off-duty… She always clings to this concept as if it were a sacred, untouchable treasure…
???? (MALE): My name is Skyler.
???? (FEMALE): And my name is also Skyler.
???? (MALE): We need to talk.
???? (FEMALE): This is very important.
SCARLETT: I see. Enter, Skylers. You’re allowed in a very limited territory. Security is absolute.
LISA: (from the distance) And I mean it!

Two SKYLERS are entering the HQ.

Change of the scene. It’s a garage of the HQ. RICK, with the wrenches on his belt and one in his hand, is performing maintenance on SAYLOR’s time machine SUV. ALICE, ever full of 19-year-old energy of a 3000s citizen, bounces around and asks infinite questions just to keep herself busy and… burn off the sugar she consumed recently.

ALICE: Ooh, what is this blue tube? Why is it glowing so brightly?
RICK: It’s a thick, concentrated, gel-like antifreeze. Before you ask if it’s Saylor’s idea of mood lighting, it’s not.
ALICE: Antifreeze? Is some component getting extremely cold during time travel?
RICK: Well, the fridge that is mounted to the trunk of this SUV has a cooling dispenser. When the fridge is active, it works as an active cooling system. Sometimes it overworks and you need to counterbalance it.
ALICE: Cooling system? Like, delivering melted ice or something?
RICK: Something like that. Cold water. If it ever turns into ice, there is a system for quick grinding and light melting.
ALICE: That’s so sophisticated…
RICK: Look. You can read the manual, okay? Open the front passenger door and rummage through the glovebox.
ALICE: I’ve read that book like three times from start to finish. It’s almost if it’s been written in an alien dialect of English. When you or Saylor explain, I understand something.

MARIE opens the garage door and enters.

MARIE: Oh. Rick. You’re here.
RICK: You. Something that doesn’t let you sleep?
MARIE: _You._

Back to the main room of HQ. SCARLETT introduces the part of the crew who refuses to sleep for some reason.

SCARLETT: My name is Scarlett. This is Saylor, Stelle, Lisa—
CHARLOTTE: (stands up to shake hands) And Codename Vicky! Stands for Victorian. Better known as Charlotte.
SCARLETT: Just Charlotte.
CHARLOTTE: Don’t kill the vibe, I tried to be charming.
SAYLOR: You always do. Sit down. (to the newcomers) And uh, you too.
SCARLETT: And they have the same name, Skyler.

Everyone sits down at the table.

SAYLOR: Two peas in a pod, how interesting. Twins?
SKYLER-M: No.
SKYLER-F: We’re married.
CHARLOTTE: (gasps quietly) No way!
STELLE: Is this normal in this century?
LISA: No. In fact, I find it difficult to search for similar precedents.
SAYLOR: Anyways. It’s 1 AM. What are you doing here? Why have you came?
SKYLER-F: We have a proposal.
SAYLOR: (to SKYLER-M) Proposal?
SKYLER-M: Ask her. I’m wondering myself.
SAYLOR: (lightly chuckles) Okay. (to SKYLER-F) What do you want to propose?
SKYLER-F: Cooperation. Against the forces of Darkrock and independent rogue sleepwalkers who stopped obeying orders and became more dangerous.
SAYLOR: Who are you?
SKYLER-M: We’re independent investigators.
SKYLER-F: Freelancers. We’re not for hire. Never were for long years.
SKYLER-M: We don’t accept sponsorships and high-class patronage.
SCARLETT: You’re not agents? Neither under government or independent?
SKYLER-M: No, we’re not.
SKYLER-F: We never engage in the offensive. We work with the information.
SCARLETT: Luckily we have a perfect match for you. Marie. We’ll let her know in the morning if you provide your intentions and where we fit in.

Garage of HQ.

MARIE: Why are you not sleeping? You’re not waiting for me exactly, right?
ALICE: Ahem. I’m also here, by the way.
MARIE: So I noticed. Must be boring for you when there’s nothing to demolish.
ALICE: The sleepwalkers will wake up in an hour. I’m simply waiting.
MARIE: Waiting is not in your vocabulary, come on. You’re incapable of sitting still for three minutes.
ALICE: Neither is in yours. Why are you constantly following Rick like a puppy?
MARIE: Let me remind you who was orbiting him like a moon for the last 24 minutes. “Ooh, Rick, what is this? Ooh, Rick, what is that?” Grab the manual!
RICK: Stop!! Marie, is this why you came here? To unnerve me while I’m trying to stay awake for half an hour?
MARIE: So Alice doesn’t unnerve you and I do. Uh-huh.
RICK: (calmly) She’s just asking questions. It’s called casual curiosity.
MARIE: Oh so _that’s_ what makes you passively invested in the conversation. I see.
RICK: (keeps maintaining) Your idea of making me invested in the conversation is bordering with manipulating me into your agenda. Which is 80% marriage proposal. I’m not buying it.
MARIE: Mm-hm. Okay. Okay, I heard you. Well, I’m _casually_ curious… What would you make of our new guests?
RICK: Guests? What are you talking about?
MARIE: Skylers. We have two new people. Both named Skyler. High chance of them becoming regular crew members.
RICK: Tell me more.
ALICE: Yeah, who are they?
MARIE: (pulls out the phone) Might just show you live CCTV footage. See for yourselves.

Main room of HQ.

SKYLER-F: Darling?
SKYLER-M: Here. A secure flash drive containing everything you might be interested in. That includes every piece of documentary work we’ve done regarding Darkrock’s infrastructure.
SKYLER-F: We even have exclusives.
LISA: So do we, but I’ll be interested in analyzing where our investigations cross and where we had blind spots. But, what about encryption and security measures?
SKYLER-F: Military-grade encryption, we use three different algorithms.
SKYLER-M: I’m willing to assist in decryption.
SKYLER-F: I bet you have isolated environment to avoid software damage.
SAYLOR: Yes, we have a room with absolutely isolated computers, they have no network connection, and operating systems are fully protected on BIOS level. It’s totally safe.
SKYLER-F: Now for the immediate briefing.
SKYLER-M: Here are some printed documents and photos of what we managed to unearth.

SAYLOR takes the folders and skims through. CHARLOTTE and STELLE are leaning in.

STELLE: Hey, I recognize this model of sleepwalkers…
CHARLOTTE: We couldn’t figure out their parameters, remember?
STELLE: But here… They’re all stated plainly.
SKYLER-M: Professional technical education.
SKYLER-F: Same for me.
SAYLOR: This is ridiculous. Are you clones or something?
SKYLER-F: No, we’ve just met in university.
SAYLOR: And you had the same name.
SKYLER-M: I’ve changed mine.
SKYLER-F: Just because we were impossibly similar.
SKYLER-M: Even though we were in different classes.
SKYLER-F: I graduated one year earlier.
SAYLOR: Stellar…
STELLE: Stellar indeed…
CHARLOTTE: So uh, okay, it’s all good. Interesting stuff, interesting people, interesting times. We were about to go home. Can we continue our conversation at 10 AM or later?
SKYLER-M: Yes, we can do that.
SCARLETT: Then we shall be waiting.
LISA: Leave the flash drive, I’ll analyze it tonight.
SKYLER-M: I’ll tell you the decryption details before we leave.
SKYLER-F: We leave documents for you as well.
SKYLER-M: We believe we have better chance of solving this Darkrock-involved crisis if we join forces.
SAYLOR: Any help would be nice. But we watch you carefully.
STELLE: You know, we don’t need problems.
SCARLETT: Return when we start a new day of operations. You’ll be needed.
SKYLER-F: We will.

Garage of HQ.

RICK: What a peculiar couple…
MARIE: Hm. These are the exact words I want others to say about us.
RICK: Don’t even dream of it.
ALICE: Well. Just two people who care about their business. Nothing special. I don’t see why they wouldn’t fit in the HQ shenanigans. They tick all the boxes.
MARIE: That’s what makes me wonder. Oh well. That’s the only thing I wanted to share. I’ve got work to do in the morning.
RICK: All of us do, you’re not special.
MARIE: I mean the 2024 agency reports! At the end of the day, I’m an external workforce here. Just like Scarlett.
RICK: No. Scarlett is rogue. You’re still hired. Don’t confuse that.
MARIE: Rogue or not, saboteur or not, she’s still officially tied to the agency. She mustn’t deny that. I’m off. See you later.

Just as MARIE was about to walk out the door, she ran into CHARLOTTE, who was entering the garage.

CHARLOTTE: Oh blooming heck. Sorry, I didn’t expect you.
MARIE: Never mind. You better go home too, it’s late.
CHARLOTTE: Fine, fine.

MARIE leaves, CHARLOTTE enters properly.

CHARLOTTE: Any success with this… tin can?
RICK: (calmly) Charley, be a bit more respectful. Saylor uses the best she’s got.
CHARLOTTE: Pity. She deserves better. Really, ditch this monstrosity, the fridge alone attracts the sleepwalkers more than it provides the substantial value.
RICK: Char. I mean it. Do you know why I don’t take you seriously?
CHARLOTTE: I wonder.
RICK: Bias. This PTT doesn’t help you to be cooperative.
CHARLOTTE: You can’t say that! It’s portable because 5 people can hold onto each other and I transport them all for the price of one!
ALICE: But this time machine has so many bells and whistles! It’s fun! Complicated, confusing, but fun.
CHARLOTTE: You don’t even understand what flux capacitor means.
ALICE: Flux-what?
CHARLOTTE: Never mind. This model doesn’t have it. (unfolds PTT and taps the touchscreen) But this—
RICK: Wait, it didn’t have the screen.
CHARLOTTE: Different model. Look. It has an app that tracks the weather in every place, every day, every century where weather forecasts exist. Past and future. Brilliant, isn’t it?
RICK: You need this only because you need to find every stormy place to charge your parasol! I bet you even mark already visited times and places to avoid stumbling upon yourself.
CHARLOTTE: (gasps) Ricky, thank you for the fantastic suggestion! I definitely need to consider logging my visits. Well, instead of memorizing them.
ALICE: You memorized? (wraps her arm around CHARLOTTE’s left arm, leaning in) How many trips do you remember?
CHARLOTTE: A lot. Only because I’m trying to make every trip unforgettable.
ALICE: (quiet shock) That’s insane. All in your head!
CHARLOTTE: Look. I use the touchscreen model for just one day. I’m still adjusting to the new traveling style, okay?
ALICE: I guess Saylor doesn’t memorize her trips.
CHARLOTTE: She’s practical. This SUV is a vehicle. This PTT is a universal key to every adventurous door. See the difference?

CHARLOTTE folds PTT, then puts her arm around RICK’s waist. RICK moves her hand away, but she puts it back. RICK rolls his eyes.

CHARLOTTE: Well, I have to learn voice control too. Imagine hands-free time travel.
ALICE: It can’t be hands-free. Your PTT is literally strapped to an arm. And you still rely on the parasol to power it up.
CHARLOTTE: But talking instead of typing! A step forward!
RICK: Everything for you to avoid having a phone.
CHARLOTTE: I mean, PTT supports calls.
RICK: Is there anything it can’t do?
CHARLOTTE: Cook. Unfortunately.
ALICE: Well. Thank you for your gadget trivia, that was interesting.
CHARLOTTE: Hey. I see you’re yawning without opening a mouth.
ALICE: True. I had to go 3 hours ago but… Every time Rick maintains something, it would be a crime to miss some short lecture. (yawn) He explains things so well.
RICK: Can you even process what I said?
ALICE: Funny that I don’t understand it now, but during the night, brain does something and I remember it perfectly, whether I want it or not. I really need a rest. It’s been a long day.
RICK: See you in the morning then.
ALICE: Bye, Rick. Charlie.
CHARLOTTE: Bye. See ya.

ALICE leaves. CHARLOTTE still keeps her hand on RICK’s waist.

CHARLOTTE: Well, my fella. You’re tired as well.
RICK: I know. Well, if not Marie, I would stay awake a little longer.
CHARLOTTE: Don’t strain yourself. You really like to validate her disruptive influence involuntarily, don’t you?
RICK: Happens. What else can I do, though. Someone has to keep her in check. To some extent.
CHARLOTTE: You don’t help.
RICK: You really suggest to leave her alone?
CHARLOTTE: And you can’t notice that? Seriously, Rick? You’re not blind, you see what happens when you give her an inch! (pulls RICK closer to her side) I think you need to redirect your energy. And you need vacation. You’re wearing thin, pal. I hate to see my close friend in this stressed state.
RICK: I don’t know, Char. Someone has to—
CHARLOTTE: Don’t say anything. Forced rest. I don’t care.
RICK: You know, every time you do this side hug, you make me nervous.
CHARLOTTE: Relax, I’m not surprise-stealing you today. PTT is inactive. (stops hugging, pats RICK on shoulder) Go home, my friend. Saylor is worried.
RICK: Right. Thanks for your time.
CHARLOTTE: My pleasure. See you in the morning.

## P.S.

STELLE: Mom. May I ask who is the leader of this HQ? Is it true that it’s not you? Or are you deceiving? Like a clever way to hide your vulnerabilities from the enemies.
SAYLOR: You’re about to find out. Am I right, Mr. Blake?
BLAKE: Saylor. It’s a shame we see each other so rarely.
SAYLOR: Well, let’s be fair, it’s purely your choice to stay two hundred yards underground. I don’t do bunkers. I prefer occasional winds and rains.
BLAKE: I trust you keep managing to bring talented people under your wing brilliantly.
SAYLOR: It amazes me that you allow me to do HR stuff freely.
BLAKE: Because _you_ are brilliant. Simple as that.
SAYLOR: You trust me _way_ too much, I’m afraid. Are you sure it won’t backfire when you expect it the least?
BLAKE: Why wouldn’t I trust Darkrock’s biggest enemy? It’s _you_ who the utterly corrupt President of Earth himself recognizes as a legitimate threat. Don’t you think it’s some kind of valid recommendation for the opposing forces?
SAYLOR: Just him being petty, that’s all. I’m not that talented. I’m just Saylor. You overrate me.
BLAKE: Your achievements speak louder than your words.
SAYLOR: Achievements… Bombing sleepwalkers. What an achievement.
BLAKE: You dismantled the most dangerous kinds. More than that, you were eager to crack their encryption and extract data straight from their hard drives. You are that magnetic force that pulls skillful people in. You possess the power of time travel to turn every situation into a win. Why wouldn’t I give you freedom? The very asset you’re actually fighting for.
SAYLOR: You leave me no words to deliver.
BLAKE: Say nothing. My trust in you is boundless. In my book, you never failed once.
SAYLOR: That’s reassuring. But really, you make me drown in compliments. I would be pleased if I heard some critique as well.
BLAKE: You won’t hear it from me. I’m sure your own circle criticizes you enough to keep you on track.
SAYLOR: Fair enough. You’re right.
STELLE: I don’t buy that. You treat Saylor like a goddess.
BLAKE: She achieves the results. That’s all I care about.
STELLE: I need more, mister.
BLAKE: Oh. Saylor, seems like you have a rival.
SAYLOR: She’s my very close relative. Stelle.
STELLE: And I need to know everything. I guess I may even ask more questions than Saylor ever asked you.
BLAKE: I’m ready for this conversation. But let’s arrange it for another time.
STELLE: Anytime you prefer. Strictly under Saylor’s guidance, since she’s an established member of the Resistance.
BLAKE: Deal.

Change of the scene. 1:40 AM. The SKYLERS’ house. It has two floors. SKYLER-M is finishing brushing his teeth. In a few seconds, SKYLER-F appears in the doorway. Both of them are wearing black tank tops and black short shorts tied with drawstrings. 

SKYLER-F: You’re taking too long.
SKYLER-M: Just one more minute.
SKYLER-F: Why are you always later than me? I don’t like your productivity.
SKYLER-M: Oh come on.

The hallway of the second floor.

SKYLER-M: (continues) Be fair. For the last week, I was later than you only about 20 times. And you know that. It’s not even catastrophic. My superiority rate was over 90% anyway.
SKYLER-F: About 20 times.
SKYLER-M: Are you trying to say it’s about 20 more than you wanted?
SKYLER-F: I just want you to improve. You’ve let yourself go.
SKYLER-M: No, I didn’t.
SKYLER-F: You think so? I’ll show you.
SKYLER-M: Please don’t. Focus on the situation, not me. I just want to sleep, Skyler, that’s all.
SKYLER-F: You _are_ the situation. (pushing forward) Go now.

Bedroom.

SKYLER-M: I’m not proving today anything. As a matter of fact, why are _you_ rushing?
SKYLER-F: Me? Rushing?
SKYLER-M: You couldn’t wait! You had to go to the HQ right at 1 AM. What’s wrong with you?
SKYLER-F: (semi-quietly) Nothing. Look. We saved a lot of time for the morning. Now they know about us, Lisa even spends her time analyzing our flash drive data right at this very moment. By breakfast, we’ll have results and even more data to use for the investigative work. We succeeded. And certainly not without _your_ help. (puts hands on SKYLER-M’s shoulders) So don’t think it’s only me who made the difference.
SKYLER-M: Next time I’m taking the lead.
SKYLER-F: Why are we so competitive anyway?
SKYLER-M: Because it helps us gain more results.
SKYLER-F: Gain more sleep. You’re clearly deprived of it.
SKYLER-M: And you’re not.
SKYLER-F: (yawning) I was only masking it. Because I wanted to finish the job first and foremost.
SKYLER-M: Workaholic. You’re impossible, do you know that?
SKYLER-F: Shut up. Sometimes you’re overworking too. Let’s not fool ourselves. Go to bed.
SKYLER-M: No, you first.
SKYLER-F: No. Both.

2:35 AM. Lights are already off, but SKYLER-F just stares at the ceiling.

SKYLER-M: (accidentally wakes up a little bit) Wh— Skyler? Why are you not sleeping?
SKYLER-F: I can’t.
SKYLER-M: What’s the matter?
SKYLER-F: The 61st Base of Darkrock.
SKYLER-M: Stop. Please. You can think about it in the morning. Okay?
SKYLER-F: No, no, can’t you understand? Sigh… Anyway.
SKYLER-M: Sleep.
SKYLER-F: Okay, tell me. Scarlett. What do you think about her?
SKYLER-M: Does it even matter?
SKYLER-F: She’s gorgeous.
SKYLER-M: Why do you even talk about her?
SKYLER-F: If I became like her, what would you think?
SKYLER-M: Really? You’re considering it?
SKYLER-F: Why not? She had so many amazing stories as a rogue agent…
SKYLER-M: Look. I respect her professionally. But you couldn’t look at her without your breath catching. You pined for her for a year and a half. Besides, you’re literally 2 years older than her. You can’t be serious.
SKYLER-F: That doesn’t make her less amazing or less powerful!
SKYLER-M: You’re literally trying to imitate her lifestyle! You think I wouldn’t notice the difference? Is that exactly why you rushed to the HQ?
SKYLER-F: I _wanted_ you to notice.
SKYLER-M: Skyler, sweetie. You have to be yourself. You don’t need to change. I didn’t ask for it.

SKYLER-M buries his face in the pillow. SKYLER-F still stares at the ceiling.
